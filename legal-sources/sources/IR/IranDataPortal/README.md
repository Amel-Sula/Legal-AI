# IR/IranDataPortal — Iran Data Portal (Syracuse University)

English translations of Iranian legal texts from [irandataportal.syr.edu](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/). Curated academic portal covering constitutional law, election law, banking regulations, labor law, and court decisions.

## Data Coverage

- **Type:** Legislation, Case Law
- **Language:** English (translations of Persian originals)
- **Coverage:** Constitutional era (1979-present)
- **Topics:** Constitution, electoral law, banking, labor, penal code, Guardian Council, judiciary
