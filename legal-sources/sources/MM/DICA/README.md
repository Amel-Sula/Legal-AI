# MM/DICA — Myanmar Directorate of Investment and Company Administration

Laws, rules, and notifications from the Myanmar Directorate of Investment and
Company Administration (DICA). Covers the Myanmar Companies Law 2017, Myanmar
Investment Law 2016, Insolvency Law 2020, Special Economic Zone Law, and
related regulations, directives, and notifications.

- **Source**: https://www.dica.gov.mm/laws-rules-and-notifications/
- **API**: WordPress REST API at `/wp-json/wl/v1/wl_doc`
- **Format**: PDF documents with extractable text
- **Language**: English (foreign-language translations filtered out)
- **Documents**: ~50 English-language laws, rules, and notifications
