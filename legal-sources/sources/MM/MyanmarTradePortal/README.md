# MM/MyanmarTradePortal

Myanmar National Trade Portal — Legal Documents section containing trade-related laws, regulations, customs acts, FDA bulletins, and export/import notifications.

## Data

- **Source**: https://www.myanmartradeportal.gov.mm/en/legals
- **Format**: HTML pages with PDF attachments
- **Languages**: English and Burmese
- **Coverage**: ~550 trade-related legal documents (laws, orders, notifications, bulletins)
- **Data types**: legislation

## Access

No authentication required. Public government trade portal.
