# ZW/RBZ-Directives — Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe Exchange Control Directives

Exchange control directives, circulars, and orders issued by the Reserve Bank of
Zimbabwe under section 35 of the Exchange Control Regulations (SI 109/1996).

Covers directives from 2019–2025 including foreign exchange guidelines, gold coin
regulations, bureau de change operations, and interbank market rules.

Note: The RBZ website HTML pages are protected by Radware Bot Manager (CAPTCHA),
but direct PDF document URLs are publicly accessible without authentication.
The scraper uses a curated list of known document URLs discovered via search engines.
