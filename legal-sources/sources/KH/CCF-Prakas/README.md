# KH/CCF-Prakas — Cambodia Competition Commission Prakas

Ministerial orders (prakas) from the Cambodia Consumer Protection, Competition, and
Fraud Repression Directorate-General (CCF/DG). Covers food safety, halal standards,
consumer protection, and competition law enforcement.

- **Source URL:** https://www.ccfdg.gov.kh/en/laws-regulations/prakas/
- **Documents:** ~28 PDFs (1999–2023)
- **Format:** PDF download from WordPress uploads
- **Language:** Khmer and English
