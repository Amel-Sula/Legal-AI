# KH/SERC-Regulations

**Securities and Exchange Regulator of Cambodia (SERC) — Prakas & Guidelines**

Capital markets regulator for Cambodia. Publishes laws, anukrets (sub-decrees),
prakas (ministerial orders), and guidelines governing the securities sector.

- **URL**: https://www.serc.gov.kh/english/
- **Data type**: legislation
- **Format**: PDF documents hosted on a PHP board system
- **Estimated size**: ~47 documents across 4 categories

## Categories

| Board ID | Category | Approx. Count |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| m21laws | Laws | 4 |
| m22Anukrets | Anukrets (Sub-decrees) | 6 |
| m23Prakas | Prakas (Ministerial Orders) | 30 |
| m24guide | Orders & Guidelines | 7 |
