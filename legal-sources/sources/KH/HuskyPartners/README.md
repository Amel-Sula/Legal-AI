# KH/HuskyPartners — Husky & Partners Cambodian Legal Library

The largest free online collection of Cambodian laws and regulations, maintained by Husky & Partners law firm. Covers Royal Decrees, Laws, Sub-Decrees, Prakas, and Circulars across 30+ legal categories from 1920 to 2024.

**URL:** https://huskyandpartners.com/huskypublication/legal-library

## Data

- **Type:** legislation
- **Coverage:** 1920–2024
- **Records:** ~640 documents (~243 with English translations)
- **Document types:** Royal Decrees, Laws, Sub-Decrees, Prakas, Circulars
- **Languages:** English (unofficial translations), Khmer
- **Full text:** Extracted from PDFs via PyMuPDF (~85% text-extractable)
