# NG/NAFDAC — Nigeria National Agency for Food and Drug Administration

NAFDAC is the Nigerian federal agency responsible for regulating and controlling
the manufacture, importation, exportation, advertisement, distribution, sale, and
use of food, drugs, cosmetics, medical devices, chemicals, and packaged water.

This source collects three categories of official NAFDAC documents:

1. **Gazetted Regulations** (47 documents) — legally binding regulations published
   in the Federal Republic of Nigeria Official Gazette, covering drugs, food,
   cosmetics, chemicals, medical devices, and veterinary products.

2. **Guidelines** (150+ documents) — official guidance documents for GMP,
   registration, inspection, labelling, clinical trials, pharmacovigilance,
   import/export, and more.

3. **Regulatory Directives** (25+ documents) — specific regulatory orders such as
   product bans, discontinuations, and policy directives.

All documents are PDF files hosted on the NAFDAC WordPress site.

## Data access

- Regulations: AJAX endpoint via Ninja Tables plugin (table_id=9904)
- Guidelines: HTML page with direct PDF links
- Regulatory Directives: HTML page with direct PDF links
- All PDFs are publicly accessible without authentication
