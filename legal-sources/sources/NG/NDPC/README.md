# NG/NDPC — Nigeria Data Protection Commission

Fetches publications from the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC),
established under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

## Data sources

1. **WordPress REST API** (`/wp-json/wp/v2/posts`) — news articles,
   enforcement announcements, compliance guidance, and press releases.
2. **Resources page PDFs** — the NDP Act, General Application and
   Implementation Directive (GAID), regulations, annual reports, guidance
   notices, and white papers.

## Document types

All records are classified as `doctrine` — official regulatory guidance,
enforcement communications, and policy publications.
