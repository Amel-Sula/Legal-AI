# TR/MFA-Treaties — Turkish International Agreements

Database of international agreements (Uluslararası Antlaşmalar) to which the
Republic of Türkiye is a party, published by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

- **URL:** https://ua.mfa.gov.tr
- **Data type:** legislation
- **Coverage:** Bilateral and multilateral treaties (~8,200 entries)
- **Full text:** Extracted from PDF attachments (ratification law + treaty annex
  published in the Resmî Gazete). Older scanned-image PDFs without OCR are skipped.

## How it works

Treaty detail pages are enumerated by sequential numeric ID (`/detay.aspx?{id}`).
Each page carries structured metadata (parties, subjects, signature date, Official
Gazette reference) and links to associated PDF files (`/files.ashx?{fileId}`).
Text is extracted from the PDFs; treaties with fewer than 1,000 characters of
extracted text are treated as scanned/empty and skipped.
