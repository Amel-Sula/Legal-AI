# SZ/ERS-TaxLegislation

**Eswatini Revenue Service — Tax Legislation & Practice Notes**

Tax legislation, amendments, VAT regulations, and double taxation agreements
published by the Eswatini Revenue Service (ERS) at ers.org.sz.

## Data

- **Primary legislation**: Revenue Authority Act 2008, Income Tax Order 1975, Customs and Excise Act 1971, VAT Act 2011, Graded Tax Act 1968, Fuel Tax Act 2022, Alcohol and Tobacco Levy Act
- **Amendments**: Income Tax Amendment Act 2023, VAT amendments 2022–2025, Compliance Certificates Regulations 2022
- **VAT Regulations**: Full VAT regulations, rate amendment regulations
- **Tax Treaties**: Double taxation agreements with South Africa, Mauritius, UK, Seychelles, Taiwan, Botswana, Lesotho

PDFs are hosted on the ERS document server (port 8000). Some PDFs are scanned
images and are skipped (no OCR). ~10 of ~21 documents have extractable text.
