# SZ/CBE-Regulations — Central Bank of Eswatini

Financial regulations, banking laws, guidelines, directives, and payment system
rules published by the Central Bank of Eswatini (CBE).

## Coverage

- **Acts**: Financial Institutions Act 2005, Money Laundering Prevention Acts
- **Constitutional**: Constitution of Swaziland 2005
- **Orders**: Central Bank Order, Exchange Control Order
- **Regulations**: UNSCR Regulations
- **Financial Regulation**: Licensing Policy
- **National Payment Systems**: Guidelines, directives, standards, rules (SWIPSS, SAECH, NPS)

## Data source

PDFs hosted on the CBE WordPress site at `centralbank.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/`.
Content scraped from legislation, financial-regulation, and national-payment-systems pages.
