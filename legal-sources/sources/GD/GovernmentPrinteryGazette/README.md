# GD/GovernmentPrinteryGazette

Grenada Government Printery Gazette — official gazette published by the
Government Printery at [gazettes.gov.gd](https://gazettes.gov.gd/).

~604 gazette issues from 2017–2026 (plus historical 1953/1983/2015).
Published weekly (Fridays) with extraordinary editions as needed.
Covers legislation, statutory rules and orders, government notices.

Distinct from GD/ParliamentGazette which covers gazette documents hosted
on the Parliament website (grenadaparliament.gd).
