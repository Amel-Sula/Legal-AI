# PS/Maqam — Palestine Laws Encyclopedia (Maqam)

Court judgments from the Palestinian legal system, hosted by An-Najah National
University. Covers decisions from the Court of Cassation (النقض), High Court of
Justice (العدل العليا), Constitutional Court, corruption courts, and other
Palestinian courts since 1994.

- **Source:** https://maqam.najah.edu
- **Records:** ~11,000 court judgments
- **Language:** Arabic
- **Format:** HTML pages with embedded text; PDF attachments
