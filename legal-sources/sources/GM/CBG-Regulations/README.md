# GM/CBG-Regulations — Central Bank of The Gambia

Financial regulations, guidelines, and acts published by the Central Bank of The Gambia (CBG).

**Coverage:** ~38 documents including prudential guidelines, insurance act/regulations, FX policy, payment system pricing, bond guidelines, CBG bills guidelines, and T-bills conditions.

**Source:** [CBG Guidelines](https://www.cbg.gm/guidelines-3) and related pages.
