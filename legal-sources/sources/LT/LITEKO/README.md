# LT/LITEKO — Lithuania Court Information System Public Decisions

All public court decisions from Lithuania's Court Information System (LITEKO),
covering all Lithuanian courts of general jurisdiction and specialised courts.

## Coverage

- **Courts**: Supreme Court of Cassation, Court of Appeal, 5 regional courts, 12 district courts
- **Period**: January 2010 – present
- **Volume**: 20+ million digitized cases
- **Content**: Anonymized court decisions, rulings, and orders with full text

## Data Access

Monthly CSV files with case metadata are published at:
- `https://liteko.teismai.lt/csv/viesi_sprendimai_YYYYMM.csv`

Full text of each decision is available at:
- `https://liteko.teismai.lt/viesasprendimupaieska/tekstas.aspx?id={doc_id}`

Historical yearly CSV archives (2010–2022) are cached on data.gov.lt:
- `https://data.gov.lt/datasets/1938/`
