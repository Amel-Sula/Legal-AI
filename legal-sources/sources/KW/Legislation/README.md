# KW/Legislation — Kuwait e-Government Laws and Regulations

Major Kuwaiti laws published as PDF files on the [e-Government portal](https://e.gov.kw/sites/kgoenglish/Pages/Business/InfoSubPages/LawsAndRegulations.aspx).

Covers 20 key laws including the Constitution, Civil Law, Commercial Law, Labor Law, Criminal Procedures, Environmental Protection, Cybercrime Law, and more. Content is in Arabic.

## Access

- **Format**: PDF downloads from static URLs
- **Auth**: None required
- **Extraction**: pdfplumber for text extraction
- **Language**: Arabic
