# KW/CBK — Central Bank of Kuwait Regulations

Banking regulations, instructions, and circulars issued by the Central Bank of Kuwait (CBK).

## Coverage

- Instructions to Conventional Banks (~40 documents)
- Instructions for Islamic Banks (~35 documents)
- Instructions for Finance Companies (~7 documents)
- Instructions for Exchange Companies
- Instructions for Credit Information Companies (~6 documents)
- Regulatory Instructions for e-Payment Services (~3 documents)

All documents are English translations of Arabic originals.

## Data Access

PDFs are publicly available on the CBK website under "Legislation and Regulation". No API or authentication required.
