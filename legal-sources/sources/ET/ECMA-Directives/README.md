# ET/ECMA-Directives — Ethiopian Capital Market Authority

Capital market directives, proclamations, and guidelines from the Ethiopian Capital Market Authority (ECMA).

- **URL:** https://ecma.gov.et/laws-regulation/
- **Data type:** legislation
- **Records:** ~15 documents (directives, proclamations, guidelines, drafts)
- **Format:** PDF (text extraction via pdfminer)
- **Method:** HTML scraping of WPDM download links from laws-regulation page

ECMA was established under Proclamation No. 1248/2021 to regulate Ethiopia's
capital markets. Documents cover securities regulation, public offering rules,
SRO recognition, AML/CFT compliance, and dematerialization of securities.
