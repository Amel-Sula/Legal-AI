# LY/LawSocietyGazette — Libya Law Society Legislation Archive

Comprehensive archive of Libyan legislation maintained by the Libyan Law Society (المجمع القانوني الليبي). Contains 19,993 documents including laws, decisions, decrees, regulations, circulars, and constitutional texts. Full text in Arabic.

- **URL**: https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/
- **Method**: HTML scraping (list pages + individual pages with full text)
- **Language**: Arabic
- **Documents**: ~19,993 (laws, decisions, decrees, regulations, circulars, constitutional)
- **Full text**: Yes, embedded directly in HTML pages
