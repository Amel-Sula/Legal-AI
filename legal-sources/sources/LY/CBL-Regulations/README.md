# LY/CBL-Regulations — Central Bank of Libya

Banking laws, financial legislation, and regulatory circulars published by the Central Bank of Libya (مصرف ليبيا المركزي).

**Coverage:** ~100+ documents — banking laws (Law No. 1 of 2005), income tax, investment, commercial activity, anti-money laundering, plus regulatory circulars (2010–2023).

**Source:** [Central Bank of Libya](https://cbl.gov.ly/en/laws/)
