# AG/ONDCP

Antigua & Barbuda AML/CFT Regulatory Library — published by the Office of
National Drug and Money Laundering Control Policy (ONDCP), the country's
AML/CFT supervisory authority, at [ondcp.gov.ag](https://ondcp.gov.ag/laws/).

Covers the Money Laundering (Prevention) Act and its many amendments, the
Proceeds of Crime Act, the ONDCP Act, the Prevention of Terrorism Act,
Friendly Societies legislation, AML/CFT regulations, supervisory guidelines,
directives, and the Harare Scheme on mutual assistance. Full text is
extracted from the official PDFs via pdfplumber.

Older statutes and directives are scanned image PDFs without a text layer;
those are skipped automatically (no OCR). Born-digital documents — most
guidelines, recent statutes, and conventions — extract cleanly.
