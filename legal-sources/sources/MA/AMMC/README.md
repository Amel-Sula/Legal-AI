# MA/AMMC — Autorité Marocaine du Marché des Capitaux

Capital markets regulatory documents from Morocco's financial markets authority (AMMC).

## Coverage

- **Circulaires**: AMMC circulars on market integrity, AML, investment advisors
- **Arrêtés**: Ministerial orders approving/homologating AMMC circulars
- **Dahirs & Lois**: Laws governing capital markets
- **Décrets**: Implementing decrees
- **Règlements généraux**: General regulations of the stock exchange
- **Autres**: Other regulatory texts

~146 PDFs total across 6 categories. ~20% have native text layers; scanned PDFs are skipped.

## Data Source

- URL: https://www.ammc.ma/fr/reglementations/circulaires
- Format: PDF documents hosted on ammc.ma
- Language: French (primary)
- Auth: None required
