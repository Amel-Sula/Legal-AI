# MA/ANRT — Morocco ANRT Telecom Regulatory Decisions

Telecom regulatory documents from Morocco's National Telecommunications Regulatory Agency (ANRT).

## Coverage

- **Décisions**: ANRT director-general decisions (103+) — suspensions, authorizations, sanctions
- **Arrêtés**: Ministerial orders on telecom regulation
- **Lois**: Telecom laws and other related legislation
- **Décrets**: Implementing decrees for telecom and other regulation

~145 PDFs total across 6 categories. Most have native text layers with full text extraction.

## Data Source

- URL: https://www.anrt.ma/en/reglementation/decisions
- Format: PDF documents hosted on anrt.ma
- Language: French (primary)
- Auth: None required
