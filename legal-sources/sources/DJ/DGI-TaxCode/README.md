# DJ/DGI-TaxCode — Djibouti Direction Generale des Impots

Tax legislation, finance laws, legal codes, and tax guides published by the Direction Generale des Impots (DGI) of Djibouti, under the Ministry of Budget.

**Coverage:** ~33 documents including the General Tax Code (CGI), Customs Code, finance laws (2001-2019), other legal codes (penal procedure, commercial, family, labor), and tax guides/notices.

**Source:** [Ministry of Budget](https://budget.gouv.dj/) — multiple pages under textes-et-lois, codes, and guides sections.
