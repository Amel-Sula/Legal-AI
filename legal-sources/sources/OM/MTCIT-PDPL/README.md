# OM/MTCIT-PDPL — Oman MTCIT Laws & Regulations

Laws and executive regulations published by the Oman Ministry of Transport,
Communications and Information Technology (MTCIT).

## Coverage

- **Data Protection**: Personal Data Protection Law (RD 6/2022), Executive Regulations
- **Cybercrime**: Cybercrime Combat Law (RD 12/2011)
- **Digital**: Electronic Transactions Law (RD 39/2025)
- **Telecom/Postal**: Telecommunications Regulation Law, Postal Services Regulation Law
- **Maritime**: Maritime Law (multiple versions/amendments), navigation, port security
- **Transport**: Land Transport Law, Civil Aviation Law
- **Regulations**: Executive regulations for PDPL, maritime safety, aviation

~20 PDF documents in English/Arabic.
