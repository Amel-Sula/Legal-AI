# SS/BOSS-Regulations

**Bank of South Sudan — Regulations & Circulars**

Regulatory documents from South Sudan's central bank (BoSS). Covers banking regulations, supervision guidelines, AML policy, licensing requirements, and prudential standards.

- **Source URL:** https://boss.gov.ss/regulations/
- **Documents:** ~25 regulatory PDFs
- **Language:** English
- **Data type:** doctrine

## How it works

1. Scrapes the regulations listing page at boss.gov.ss/regulations/
2. Extracts all PDF URLs from the page
3. Downloads PDFs and extracts text with pdfplumber (fallback to PyMuPDF)
4. Normalizes into standard schema
