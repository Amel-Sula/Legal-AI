# SS/MoJCA — South Sudan Ministry of Justice Laws

Official collection of Acts and laws of the Republic of South Sudan, published
by the Ministry of Justice and Constitutional Affairs (MoJCA).

**Source URL:** https://mojca.gov.ss/laws-of-the-republic-of-south-sudan/

## Coverage

- ~77 Acts of South Sudan (2005–2017)
- Includes: Penal Code, Labour Act, Banking Act, Land Act, Constitution,
  Civil Procedure, Code of Evidence, Child Act, Companies Act, etc.
- All documents are PDFs hosted on the MoJCA WordPress site

## Data Types

- `legislation` — Acts, Codes, Regulations
