# CG/SGG-TextesOfficiels

Congo-Brazzaville official legal texts from the Secrétariat Général du Gouvernement (SGG).

**URL:** https://sgg.cg/droit-congolais/recherche.html
**Data types:** legislation
**Language:** French

Covers laws (lois), decrees (décrets), ordinances (ordonnances), and decisions published in the Republic of Congo. Documents are available as individual PDFs with selectable text. The scraper focuses on entries that have dedicated PDF links (as opposed to JO-only entries).
