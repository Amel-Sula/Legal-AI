# CG/AssembleeNationale — Congo-Brazzaville Official Journal

Republic of Congo (Congo-Brazzaville) Journal Officiel published by the
Secrétariat Général du Gouvernement (SGG).

## Source

- **URL**: https://www.sgg.cg/fr/journal-officiel/le-journal-officiel.html
- **Publisher**: Secrétariat Général du Gouvernement, République du Congo
- **Coverage**: 1,833 issues (1946–2026); text-extractable from ~2010
- **Language**: French
- **Format**: PDF (full text extractable via pdfplumber)
- **Update frequency**: Weekly (every Thursday)

## Data

Each record is one Journal Officiel issue containing laws, ordinances, decrees,
and ministerial orders. The full text of the PDF is extracted and stored.

## License

Public Domain (Government) — Official legal texts of the Republic of Congo are
not subject to copyright under Congolese intellectual property law. The site
itself notes that "only the paper version is authoritative" but does not restrict
reproduction of the digital texts.
