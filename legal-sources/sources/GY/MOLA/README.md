# Laws of Guyana (Ministry of Legal Affairs)

**Source:** [https://mola.gov.gy/laws-of-guyana](https://mola.gov.gy/laws-of-guyana)
**Country:** GY
**Data types:** legislation
**Status:** Complete

## Description

Consolidated Laws of Guyana organized by volume and chapter, published by
the Ministry of Legal Affairs. Covers ~460 acts including constitutional,
civil, criminal, commercial, and administrative law. English language.
