# GY/BOG-Regulations — Bank of Guyana

Legislation, supervision guidelines, insurance/pension guidelines, and circulars
from the Bank of Guyana.

## Coverage

- **Legislation**: ~50 acts and regulations (AML/CFT, Financial Institutions, Insurance, Credit Reporting, Money Transfer, National Payments, etc.)
- **Financial Institutions Guidelines**: 17 supervision guidelines
- **Insurance Guidelines**: 9 guidelines
- **Pension Guidelines**: 1 guideline
- **Circulars**: 5 circulars

All documents are PDFs hosted on bankofguyana.org.gy. Text is extracted via pdfminer.
