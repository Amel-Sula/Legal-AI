# WS/AG-ConsolidatedLaws

Samoa Attorney General — Consolidated Laws of Samoa 2023

## Overview

Official consolidated legislation of the Independent State of Samoa, published by the Office of the Attorney General. Contains ~267 Acts and Ordinances spanning from 1908 to 2021, consolidated as of 2023.

## Data Source

- **URL**: https://www.ag.gov.ws/consolidation-of-laws-of-samoa/
- **Format**: PDF documents (direct download, no auth)
- **Language**: English
- **Coverage**: All consolidated Acts and Ordinances of Samoa
