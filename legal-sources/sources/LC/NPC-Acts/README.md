# LC/NPC-Acts

Saint Lucia Acts of Parliament — published by the National Printing
Corporation at [npc.govt.lc](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/acts).

500-800+ Acts of Parliament from 2003–2026, organized by year. Covers
principal and amendment Acts across all areas of Saint Lucian law:
taxation, companies, immigration, citizenship, insolvency, intellectual
property, appropriation, and more. Full text is extracted from the
official PDFs via pdfplumber.
