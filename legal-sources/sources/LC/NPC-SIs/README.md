# LC/NPC-SIs

Saint Lucia Statutory Instruments — published by the National Printing
Corporation at [npc.govt.lc](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/si).

~2,000-3,000+ statutory instruments from 2001–2026, organized by year.
Covers tax orders, price controls, fiscal incentives, tourism development
orders, licensing, proclamations, and subsidiary legislation.
