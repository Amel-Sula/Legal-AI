# LC/Gazette — Saint Lucia Government Gazette

Official Government Gazette of Saint Lucia, published weekly by the National
Printing Corporation (NPC). Archive spans 2004–present (~22 years).

Contains statutory instruments, government notices, public service appointments,
tax orders, and other official publications.

## Source

- **URL:** https://npc.govt.lc/gazettes
- **Format:** PDF (text-based, extractable)
- **Coverage:** 2004–present, weekly issues
- **Organization:** By year and month
