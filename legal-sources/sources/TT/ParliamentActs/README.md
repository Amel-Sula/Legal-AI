# TT/ParliamentActs — Trinidad & Tobago Parliament Acts

Full text of Acts of Parliament of Trinidad and Tobago from 1999 to present,
published by the Parliament of the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago.

- **Source**: https://www.ttparliament.org/publications/acts-of-parliament/
- **Type**: legislation
- **Format**: PDF (text-extractable)
- **Coverage**: ~614 acts, 1999–present
- **Update frequency**: As new acts are passed

## Strategy

1. Parse WordPress sitemaps to discover all publication URLs
2. Filter for act-related URLs (containing "act" in the slug)
3. Visit each page to extract title and PDF download link
4. Download PDF and extract full text via pdfminer

## Overlap

May partially overlap with TT/DigitalLegislativeLibrary (laws.gov.tt), which
covers revised consolidated laws from 1838. This source provides the original
enacted versions as published by Parliament.
