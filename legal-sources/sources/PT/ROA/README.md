# PT/ROA — Revista da Ordem dos Advogados

Legal doctrine articles from the official journal of the **Portuguese Bar Association** (Ordem dos Advogados). Published since 1941, with 2 issues per year.

## Coverage

- **Period**: 1941–present
- **Content**: Legal theory (doutrina), annotated case law (jurisprudência comentada), editorials
- **Format**: Individual article PDFs, freely accessible
- **Volume**: ~10–15 articles per issue, ~20–30 per year

## Data Access

- Year index: `https://portal.oa.pt/publicacoes/revista-da-ordem-dos-advogados/`
- Issue pages list articles with PDF links at `/media/{id}/{author-slug}.pdf`
- No API — HTML scraping of index + issue pages, then PDF download/extraction
