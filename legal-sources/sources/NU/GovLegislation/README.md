# NU/GovLegislation — Niue Government Official Legislation

Official consolidated laws, constitution, supplements, and individual acts/regulations
published by the Government of Niue at [gov.nu](https://www.gov.nu/information/).

## Coverage

- **Consolidated Laws** (4 volumes, as at 31 December 2019)
- **Constitution of Niue** and associated documents
- **Legislation Supplements** (Acts 334-349+, 2016-2019)
- **Individual Acts** (Family Relationships Act 2022, Seal of Niue Act 2021, etc.)
- **Regulations** (Vagahau Niue Regulations 2016, Pensions amendments 2025, etc.)

## Data Format

All documents are PDFs. Text is extracted using pdfplumber.
Some newer individual acts are scanned images without OCR text layers and are skipped.
