# MW/RBM — Reserve Bank of Malawi Financial Sector Regulations

Financial sector regulations from the Reserve Bank of Malawi (RBM), covering Banking directives and guidelines, Capital Markets & Microfinance, Pensions & Insurance, and Consumer Protection.

~200 PDF documents fetched via `GetContentFile` endpoint. Full text extracted from PDFs.
