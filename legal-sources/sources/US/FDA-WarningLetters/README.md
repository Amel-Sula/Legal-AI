# US/FDA-WarningLetters

FDA Warning Letters — compliance enforcement letters issued by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration.

- **Records**: ~3,515 warning letters
- **Type**: doctrine
- **Full text**: Yes, full letter body from individual detail pages
- **Access method**: Playwright (Akamai bot protection blocks standard HTTP clients)

## Data

Warning letters are issued when the FDA finds that a manufacturer has significantly violated FDA regulations. Letters include detailed descriptions of violations, regulatory citations, and required corrective actions.

Fields: company name, issue date, issuing office (CDER, CBER, Human Foods, etc.), subject, recipient, delivery method, product type, MARCS-CMS ID.
