# US/NRC-Decisions

Nuclear Regulatory Commission adjudicatory decisions — Commission orders (CLI-*) and Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel orders (LBP-*).

- **Source**: https://www.nrc.gov/about-nrc/regulatory/adjudicatory.html
- **Type**: case_law
- **Records**: ~750 decisions (1999–present)
- **Format**: PDF documents extracted via pdfplumber
- **Access**: Direct HTTP, no authentication required

## Coverage

- **Commission Orders (CLI)**: ~536 decisions (1999–2026) on nuclear facility licensing, enforcement, and regulatory matters
- **ASLBP Orders (LBP)**: ~213 decisions (2011–2026) from the Atomic Safety and Licensing Board Panel
