# US/CPSC-Enforcement

Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) — Product Recall & Enforcement Actions

Fetches CPSC product recall records via the SaferProducts.gov REST API. Covers mandatory and voluntary recalls, consent agreements, and enforcement actions since 1973.

- **Records**: ~9,800+ recalls
- **API**: `saferproducts.gov/RestWebServices/Recall` (no auth, JSON)
- **Content**: Product descriptions, hazard details, remedy instructions
