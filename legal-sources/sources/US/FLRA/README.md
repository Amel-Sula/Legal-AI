# US/FLRA

Federal Labor Relations Authority (FLRA) Authority Decisions — adjudicates federal labor-management disputes.

- **Records**: ~10,440 authority decisions
- **Type**: case_law
- **Full text**: Yes, from individual HTML decision pages
- **Access method**: Plain HTTP requests to flra.gov (no authentication needed)

## Data

The FLRA resolves disputes under the Federal Service Labor-Management Relations Statute. Authority decisions cover unfair labor practices, representation matters, arbitration exceptions, negotiability disputes, and other case types.

Fields: parties (title), citation, case number, issuance date, arbitrator, full decision text.
