# BD/SCOB — Supreme Court Online Bulletin

The Supreme Court Online Bulletin (SCOB) is the official law report published by the Supreme Court of Bangladesh. It contains curated judgments from both the Appellate Division and High Court Division, spanning 20 volumes from 2015 to 2025 (~456 cases total).

**URL:** https://www.supremecourt.gov.bd/web/?page=bulletin.php&menu=10

## Data

- **Type:** case_law
- **Coverage:** 2015–2025 (Volumes 1–20)
- **Divisions:** Appellate Division, High Court Division
- **Records:** ~456 curated judgments
- **Language:** English (primary), Bengali
- **Full text:** Extracted from PDF judgments via PyMuPDF
