# RS/Poverenik — Serbia Commissioner for Information & Data Protection

Decisions from the Serbian Commissioner for Information of Public Importance
and Personal Data Protection (Повереник за информације од јавног значаја и
заштиту података о личности).

**Source:** https://praksa.poverenik.rs
**Coverage:** 2015–present
**Volume:** ~500–800 published practice decisions
**Language:** Serbian (Cyrillic)

## Data Types

- **case_law** — FOI complaints, data protection decisions, harmonization opinions

## Three Domains

1. **Freedom of Information (FOI)** — complaints about denied information requests
2. **Data Protection** — personal data protection enforcement
3. **Harmonization** — opinions on codes of conduct, recommendations
