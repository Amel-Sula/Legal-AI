# RS/NBS — Serbia National Bank Regulations

Regulations, decisions, and guidelines from the National Bank of Serbia (NBS),
covering all areas of central bank regulatory authority.

## Coverage

- **Categories**: Monetary policy, bank supervision, insurance, payment systems,
  digital assets, AML, financial leasing, pension funds, consumer protection,
  accounting, statistics, and more
- **Volume**: 300+ regulatory documents across 20+ categories
- **Format**: PDF documents with English translations
- **Content**: Full text extracted from official PDF publications

## Data Access

Regulation index pages by category at:
- `https://www.nbs.rs/en/drugi-nivo-navigacije/propisi/propisi-{code}/`

PDF documents at:
- `https://www.nbs.rs/documents-eng/propisi/propisi-{code}/{filename}.pdf`
