# DZ/COSOB — Algeria Securities & Exchange Commission

Regulatory texts from COSOB (Commission d'Organisation et de Surveillance des Opérations de Bourse), Algeria's securities market regulator.

## Coverage

- **Laws & ordinances** — founding legislation (Décret législatif n°93-10, etc.)
- **Executive decrees** — implementation decrees
- **Arrêtés** — ministerial orders (accounting plans for IOBs and OPCVMs)
- **Regulations & instructions** — COSOB's own rules for market operations, trading, custody, compliance
- **AML/CFT texts** — anti-money laundering laws, COSOB instructions, CTRF instructions, guidelines
- **Guidelines** — practical guides for regulated entities

## Data source

All documents are PDF files hosted on the COSOB WordPress site at `cosob.dz/wp-content/uploads/`. Full text is extracted using pdfplumber.
