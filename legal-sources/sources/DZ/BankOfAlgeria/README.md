# DZ/BankOfAlgeria — Bank of Algeria Regulations & Instructions

Banking regulations, instructions, prudential norms, AML rules, forex regulations, and monetary policy instruments published by the Bank of Algeria (Banque d'Algérie).

## Coverage

- ~143 regulatory PDF documents
- Categories: prudential norms, AML/CFT, forex, monetary policy, payment systems, financial stability, Islamic finance, accounting rules, banking conditions
- Languages: French (official)
- Period: 1990s to present

## Data access

HTML scraping of regulatory subpages on bank-of-algeria.dz, with PDF download and text extraction via pdfplumber. The main regulatory page links to ~24 thematic subpages, each containing PDF links to individual regulations.

[Open Government Data](https://www.bank-of-algeria.dz/cadre-legislatif-et-reglementaire/) — official regulatory documents published by Algeria's central bank. No explicit license stated; treated as open government data.
