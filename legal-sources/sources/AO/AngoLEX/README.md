# AngoLEX — Angolan Legislation Portal

**Source:** [angolex.com](https://angolex.com/)
**Country:** AO
**Data types:** legislation
**Status:** Complete

Comprehensive Angolan legislation portal covering Constitution, codes, laws,
presidential decrees, executive decrees, dispatches, regulations, resolutions,
notices, and international agreements. ~2,378 entries with full text in Portuguese.
