# YE/CBY-Regulations — Central Bank of Yemen Circulars & Banking Laws

Banking supervision circulars and foundational banking laws from the Central Bank
of Yemen (Aden branch). Includes ~15 regulatory circulars with full text and
7 major banking law PDFs.

**Source:** https://english.cby-ye.com

## Data

- **Circulars:** Banking supervision directives (credit classification, capital
  ratios, liquidity requirements, compliance, etc.)
- **Laws:** Central Bank Law, Banking Law, Exchange Law, Public Debt Law,
  Islamic Banks Law, AML/CFT Law
- **Language:** Arabic
- **Coverage:** 1996–present
