# Yemen Ministry of Justice Legislation Database

**Source:** [https://www.moj.gov.ye](https://www.moj.gov.ye)
**Country:** YE
**Data types:** legislation
**Status:** Complete

Laws and regulations from the Yemen Ministry of Justice and Human Rights.
PDF documents with text extraction via pdfplumber/PyPDF2/PyMuPDF.

- **Laws:** ~38 legislative documents
- **Types:** Presidential decrees, laws, regulations, executive orders
- **Language:** Arabic
- **Full text:** Yes, extracted from PDFs
