# CM/PRC — Cameroon Official Acts (Presidency)

**Source:** [https://www.prc.cm/en/news/the-acts](https://www.prc.cm/en/news/the-acts)
**Country:** CM (Cameroon)
**Data types:** legislation
**Status:** Complete

Official acts published by the Presidency of the Republic of Cameroon:
laws, decrees, ordinances, orders, decisions, and circulars.

Bilingual site (French/English). Documents are published as PDFs.
Text is extracted via PyMuPDF/pdfplumber; some older PDFs are scanned images
with limited extractable text.

Estimated volume: ~940 documents across 6 categories.
