# BH/MIA-Gazette — Bahrain Official Gazette

Official Gazette of the Kingdom of Bahrain, published by the Ministry of Information Affairs (MIA) and hosted on the Legislation and Legal Opinion Commission (LLOC) portal.

- **Source**: https://www.lloc.gov.bh/en/OfficialGazette
- **Format**: PDF (text extracted with PyMuPDF)
- **Coverage**: Issues 2917–3884+ (sequential numbering)
- **Language**: Arabic (with some English content)
- **Content**: Laws, decrees, royal orders, ministerial decisions, official announcements
