# TM/CBT-BankingLaws

Central Bank of Turkmenistan — Banking Laws & Regulations

Full text of banking laws and regulations published on the Central Bank of
Turkmenistan website (cbt.tm). Content available in English and Turkmen.

## Coverage

- Law on Central Bank of Turkmenistan (2011)
- Law on Lending Institutions and Banking (2011)
- Law on Foreign Exchange Regulation and Control (2011)
- Law on Microfinance Institutions and Microfinance (2011, Turkmen only)
- Law on Credit Unions (Turkmen only)
- Regulation on Bank Accounts (Turkmen only)
- Regulation on Non-Cash Settlements (Turkmen only)
- Procedure for Mortgage Loans (Turkmen only)
- Procedure for Cash Operations (Turkmen only)
