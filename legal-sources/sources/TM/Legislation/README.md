# TM/Legislation — Turkmenistan Parliament (Mejlis) Legislation

Official legislation from the Parliament of Turkmenistan (Mejlis), scraped from [mejlis.gov.tm](https://mejlis.gov.tm).

## Coverage

- **Laws**: ~700 laws (1991–present), paginated across 42 pages
- **Codes**: 9 major codes (Civil, Criminal, Administrative, Tax, Customs, etc.)
- **Constitutional Laws**: Constitutional amendments and related legislation

## Data Access

HTML scraping of the official parliament website. Individual law pages serve full text directly in HTML.

- List pages: `/laws/{page}?lang=tm`, `/codes?lang=tm`, `/constitutional-law/{page}?lang=tm`
- Detail pages: `/single-law/{id}?lang=tm`, `/single-code/{id}?lang=tm`

## Language

Content is primarily in **Turkmen** (Cyrillic/Latin script). Russian translations available via `?lang=ru`.
