# TM/MinjustAdalat — Turkmenistan Ministry of Justice Legal Information Center

State-registered normative legal acts from the **Hukuk Maglumatlar Merkezi** (Legal Information Center) of the Ministry of Justice of Turkmenistan.

## Coverage

- **Constitution** of Turkmenistan
- **Constitutional laws**
- **Codes** (Civil, Criminal, Tax, etc.)
- **Laws** of Turkmenistan
- **State-registered normative acts**

~356 documents. Titles in Turkmen, Russian, and English. Full text PDFs in Turkmen and Russian.

## Data Access

- **API**: `POST https://minjust.gov.tm/api/front/laws/search` returns JSON with document metadata
- **PDFs**: Freely downloadable at `https://minjust.gov.tm/assets/files/law_documents/hukuknama_{id}_{lang}.pdf`
- **Full text**: Extracted from PDFs using pdfplumber
