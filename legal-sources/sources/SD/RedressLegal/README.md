# SD/RedressLegal — Sudan Legal Resources (REDRESS)

Key Sudanese laws compiled by [REDRESS](https://redress.org/), a UK-based human rights NGO.
~80+ laws in bilingual PDF (Arabic/English) covering criminal law, constitutional law,
transitional justice, peace agreements, and institutional laws from 1925–2023.

## Data

- **Type:** legislation
- **Format:** PDF (text extracted via pdfplumber)
- **Languages:** English, Arabic
- **Coverage:** 1925–2023
- **Records:** ~67 unique laws with extractable text

## Categories

- Criminal Laws
- Institutional Laws
- Constitutional Laws
- Transitional Justice / Transitional Government
- Land
- Miscellaneous
- Regulations and Decrees
