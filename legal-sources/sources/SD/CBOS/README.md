# SD/CBOS — Central Bank of Sudan

Laws, regulations, circulars, and monetary policy documents published by the
Central Bank of Sudan (CBOS) at [cbos.gov.sd](https://cbos.gov.sd/en).

## Content

- **Laws**: Banking Business Act 2003, AML/CFT Act, Electronic Transactions Act 2007, etc.
- **Circulars**: Financial institution circulars, AML/CFT guidance, FX operations orders
- **Policies**: Annual monetary policy documents (2007-2012)
- **Languages**: English (laws, some circulars) and Arabic (FX circulars, policies)

The site is Drupal 7. Full text is extracted from HTML pages. Pages that only
contain PDF links (no inline text) are skipped — approximately 7 regulations are
PDF-only and not included.
