# Bosnia OHR Laws Database

**Source:** [https://www.ohr.int/laws-of-bih/](https://www.ohr.int/laws-of-bih/)
**Country:** BA (Bosnia and Herzegovina)
**Data types:** legislation
**Status:** Complete

## Description

Bosnia and Herzegovina legislation from the Office of the High Representative (OHR).
~504 PDFs across 12 categories: constitutions, criminal legislation, election law,
judicial/prosecutorial system, succession of the Former SFRY, human rights, public
institutions, public information, citizenship, taxation/financial legislation,
police, and defence. English translations of official texts.
