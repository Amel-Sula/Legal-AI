# ZA/ZASCA-HF — South Africa Supreme Court of Appeal Judgments

South African Supreme Court of Appeal (SCA) judgments from the
[dsfsi/zasca-sum](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dsfsi/zasca-sum) HuggingFace dataset.

- **Coverage**: ~2,000+ SCA judgments spanning 18+ years
- **Language**: English
- **Data type**: case_law
- **Full text**: Yes — complete judgment text (2K–391K chars per record)
