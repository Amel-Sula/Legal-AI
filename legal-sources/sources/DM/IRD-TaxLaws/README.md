# DM/IRD-TaxLaws — Dominica Inland Revenue Division Tax Laws & Guides

**Source:** [https://ird.gov.dm/tax-laws](https://ird.gov.dm/tax-laws)
**Data types:** legislation, doctrine

Tax guidance, policy documentation, and downloadable guides from Dominica's
Inland Revenue Division. Covers corporate income tax, excise tax, PAYE,
personal income tax, VAT, withholding tax, travel tax, licensing requirements,
past taxes, and international tax (AEOI, FATCA, CbCR, MAP).
