# DM/FSU — Dominica Financial Services Unit Legislation

**Source:** [https://fsu.gov.dm/legislation](https://fsu.gov.dm/legislation)
**Data types:** legislation

Curated collection of ~27 financial services regulatory statutes published by the
Dominica Financial Services Unit. Covers insurance, offshore banking, money laundering
prevention, virtual asset business, international business companies, tax information
exchange, and related legislation.
