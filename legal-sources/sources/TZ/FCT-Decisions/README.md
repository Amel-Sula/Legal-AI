# TZ/FCT-Decisions — Fair Competition Tribunal Case Digest

Tanzania's Fair Competition Tribunal (FCT) decisions from the official Case Digest (2007–2020).

The FCT is established under s. 83 of the Fair Competition Act No. 8 of 2003 and hears appeals from the Fair Competition Commission (FCC) and regulatory authorities (EWURA, TCRA, SUMATRA, etc.).

**Data source:** Official FCT Case Digest PDF published at [fct.or.tz](https://www.fct.or.tz/publications/decided-cases-digest). Individual decision PDFs are scanned images without extractable text; the digest provides substantive case summaries including facts, issues, holdings, and orders.

**Coverage:** ~107 cases, 2007–2020.
