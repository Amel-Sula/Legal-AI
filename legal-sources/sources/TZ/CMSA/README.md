# TZ/CMSA — Tanzania Capital Markets and Securities Authority

Fetches legislation and regulations published by the Tanzania Capital Markets
and Securities Authority (CMSA).

## Data sources

PDFs from the CMSA publications pages, organized into categories:
- **Acts** (`/publications/1`) — Capital Markets and Securities Act + amendments
- **Regulations** (`/publications/12`) — 20+ CMS regulations (licensing,
  collective investment schemes, conduct of business, etc.)
- **EAC Directives** (`/publications/15`) — East African Community Council
  Directives on capital markets
- **Publications** (`/publications/11`) — guides and brochures
