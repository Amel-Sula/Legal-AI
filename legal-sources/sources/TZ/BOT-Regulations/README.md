# TZ/BOT-Regulations

**Bank of Tanzania — Regulations, Circulars & Guidelines**

Regulatory documents from Tanzania's central bank (BoT). Covers banking acts, regulations, circulars, guidelines, procedures, and codes of conduct for financial sector supervision.

- **Source URL:** https://www.bot.go.tz/BankSupervision/Regulations
- **Documents:** ~115 regulatory PDFs
- **Language:** English, Swahili
- **Data types:** legislation, doctrine

## How it works

1. Scrapes the regulations listing page at bot.go.tz/BankSupervision/Regulations
2. Extracts all PDF links, categorizes by type (Acts, Regulations, Circulars, etc.)
3. Downloads PDFs and extracts text with pdfplumber (fallback to PyMuPDF)
4. Normalizes into standard schema
