# VC/GazetteVC — St Vincent & Grenadines Government Gazette

Official Government Gazette published by the Ministry of Legal Affairs of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. Contains new legislation, statutory rules and orders, government notices, and proclamations.

**Coverage:** 2016–present (~50–75 issues per year)
**Format:** PDF gazette issues
**Language:** English

## Data source

The gazette is published at [legal.gov.vc](https://legal.gov.vc/legal/index.php/publications) and organized by year. Each year has a category page listing individual gazette issues as downloadable PDFs.

PDF URL pattern: `/legal/images/PDF/{YYYY}Gazettes/Gazette_No_{N}_{YYYY}.pdf`

Some issues are designated "Extra-Ordinary" with filename suffix `_Extra-Ordinary`.
