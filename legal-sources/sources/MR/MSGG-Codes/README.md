# MR/MSGG-Codes — Mauritania Consolidated Legal Codes

16 consolidated legal codes from the Secrétariat Général du Gouvernement of
the Islamic Republic of Mauritania.

- **Source:** https://msgg.gov.mr/fr/droit-mauritanien/les-codes-consolides.html
- **Coverage:** 16 codes (2000–2025)
- **Language:** French
- **Data type:** legislation
- **Method:** Direct PDF download + text extraction (pdfminer)
