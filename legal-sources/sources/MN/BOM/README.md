# MN/BOM — Bank of Mongolia Regulations

English translations of Bank of Mongolia (Mongol Bank) regulatory documents.

## Coverage

- **Central Bank Law**: Law of Mongolia on Central Bank
- **Banking Supervision**: Prudential ratios, AML/CFT, corporate governance, asset classification
- **Monetary Policy**: Reserve requirements, repo, overnight deposit, bills trading
- **Forex Regulation**: Currency settlements, exchange rates, swap agreements
- **Payment Systems**: Inter-bank payments, national payment system strategy

~35 regulatory documents with English translations.

## Data Access

Content is scraped from the English-language section of mongolbank.mn. Regulation pages
(`/en/r/{id}`) contain PDF download links; text is extracted via pdfplumber.
