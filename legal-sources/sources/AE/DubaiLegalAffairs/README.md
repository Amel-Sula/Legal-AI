# AE/DubaiLegalAffairs — Dubai Official Gazette

The Dubai Official Gazette is published by the Legal Affairs Department of the
Government of Dubai. It contains all legislation issued by the Government of
Dubai since 1950, including laws, decrees, resolutions, regulations, bylaws,
instructions, and orders.

Each gazette issue is a PDF document in Arabic. The scraper downloads the PDFs
and extracts full text using pdfplumber.

- **Source**: https://legal.dubai.gov.ae/en/Services/Pages/Official-Gazette.aspx
- **Language**: Arabic
- **Coverage**: 1950–present (~769 issues)
- **Data type**: Legislation (gazette issues)
