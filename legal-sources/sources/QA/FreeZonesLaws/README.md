# QA/FreeZonesLaws — Qatar Free Zones Authority Laws & Regulations

Laws and regulations governing the Qatar Free Zones, published by the
Qatar Free Zones Authority (QFZ) at [qfz.gov.qa](https://qfz.gov.qa/).

Covers the enabling law (Law No. 34 of 2005 as amended), plus secondary
regulations on companies, licensing, land use, building permits, health
& safety, collateral & security, and online intermediary liability.
