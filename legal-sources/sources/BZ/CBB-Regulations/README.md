# BZ/CBB-Regulations — Central Bank of Belize Laws & Regulations

Banking legislation, amendments, regulations, practice directions, and AML/CFT
guidelines from the Central Bank of Belize.

**Source URL:** https://www.centralbank.org.bz/about-the-bank/laws-and-regulations

## Coverage

11 legislative categories with ~119 PDF documents:

- Central Bank of Belize Act (+ amendments)
- Domestic Banks & Financial Institutions Act (+ amendments, practice directions)
- International Banking Act (+ amendments, regulations)
- Credit Unions Act (+ amendments)
- Money Laundering & Terrorism (Prevention) Act (+ amendments, guidelines)
- Exchange Control Act (+ regulations, notices)
- Treasury Bill Act (+ rules)
- National Payment System Act (+ regulations)
- Moneylenders Act
- Deposit Insurance Act
- Credit Reporting Act

## Strategy

1. Scrape each category page for PDF links
2. Download PDFs and extract full text with pdfplumber
3. Extract dates from titles/filenames where possible
