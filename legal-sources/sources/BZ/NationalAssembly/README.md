# BZ/NationalAssembly — Belize National Assembly Acts

Acts of Parliament from the National Assembly of Belize (2015-2026).
Sessional acts published as PDFs with extractable text.

## Coverage

- **Years:** 2015-2026
- **Documents:** ~340 Acts of Parliament
- **Language:** English
- **Format:** PDF (text-extractable)

## Data Source

Individual year pages at:
```
https://www.nationalassembly.gov.bz/acts-of-parliament/acts-parliament-{YEAR}/
```

Each page lists acts with PDF download links.

## Relationship to BZ/AGMLaws

BZ/AGMLaws covers:
- Consolidated substantive laws (R.E. 2020)
- Subsidiary laws (R.E. 2020)
- Acts and Statutory Instruments 2021-2026

This source (BZ/NationalAssembly) adds:
- Sessional Acts 2015-2020 not in AGM consolidated edition
