# XK/CBK-Regulations — Kosovo Central Bank Regulations

Banking regulations, laws, and instructions issued by the Central Bank of the
Republic of Kosovo (CBK/BQK). Covers banking supervision, payment systems,
insurance, pensions, AML/CFT, crypto-assets, and financial stability.

## Data access

The bqk-kos.org HTML pages are behind Cloudflare JS challenge, but PDF documents
under `/wp-content/uploads/` are directly accessible. Regulation PDF links are
discovered via Wayback Machine cached category pages, supplemented by a curated
seed list.

## Categories

- Banks (capital adequacy, credit risk, liquidity, large exposures)
- Banking Operations (cash operations, standard account numbering)
- Insurance (premium structure, mergers, delegation, risk retention)
- Pensions (reporting, transfers, beneficiary determination, external audit)
- Payment Systems (technical standards, SCA, interbank payments)
- NBFI/MFI (registration, effective interest rates)
- AML/CFT (prevention of money laundering and terrorist financing)
- Crypto-Assets (licensing of crypto-asset service operators)
