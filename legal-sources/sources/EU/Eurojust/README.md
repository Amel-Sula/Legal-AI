# EU/Eurojust — Case Law Analysis & Judicial Cooperation Reports

Eurojust is the EU Agency for Criminal Justice Cooperation. This source
collects their published case-law analysis reports covering:

- European Arrest Warrant (EAW) CJEU case law
- European Investigation Order (EIO) case law
- Ne bis in idem principle
- Cybercrime Judicial Monitor
- Intellectual Property crime case law
- Joint Investigation Teams (JIT)
- Mutual Legal Assistance (MLA)
- Counter-terrorism judicial analysis

~70 publications scraped from the paginated listing at
`/term/case-law-analysis`, with full text extracted from PDF downloads.
