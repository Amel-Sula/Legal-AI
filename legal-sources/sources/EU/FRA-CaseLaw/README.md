# EU/FRA-CaseLaw — EU Fundamental Rights Agency Case Law Database

CJEU and ECtHR case law referencing the EU Charter of Fundamental Rights,
plus selected national court decisions. Approximately 1,900 cases.

Each case includes key facts, legal reasoning extracts, charter article
references, ECLI identifiers, and links to full judgments on EUR-Lex/HUDOC.

**Source:** https://fra.europa.eu/en/case-law-database
