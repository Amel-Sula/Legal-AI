# AI/FSCLegislation — Anguilla Financial Services Commission Legislation

Financial services legislation from Anguilla's FSC.

## Coverage

- Financial Services Commission Act and regulations
- Digital Asset Business Act (2023) and regulations
- Anguilla Utility Token Offering Act and regulations
- AML/CFT legislation
- Offshore Banking Act
- Trust and Corporate Services
- Insurance, Mutual Funds, Money Services
- Co-operative Societies
- ~64 legislative documents

## Data access

HTML listing page at `fsc.org.ai/legislation.php` with links to
`docviewer.php?doc={id}`. Each docviewer page embeds a PDF from
`documents/Document Library/Legislation/{name}.pdf`. Text extracted
using pdfplumber.
