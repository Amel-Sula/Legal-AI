# GA/CourConstitutionnelle — Cour Constitutionnelle du Gabon

Constitutional Court of Gabon — decisions on constitutionality of laws, election disputes, and institutional matters. Full text published in the Journal Officiel de la République Gabonaise.

- **Source:** https://journal-officiel.ga
- **Data type:** case_law
- **Language:** French
- **Coverage:** 2010–present (~100-300 decisions)
- **Auth:** None (open public access)

## Strategy

Decisions are published in the Journal Officiel at sequential URL IDs. The scraper filters for CC decisions by detecting "Cour Constitutionnelle" content and decision numbers matching the `N°XXX/CC` pattern.
