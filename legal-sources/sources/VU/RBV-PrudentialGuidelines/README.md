# VU/RBV-PrudentialGuidelines — Reserve Bank of Vanuatu

Prudential guidelines and supporting legislation published by the Reserve Bank
of Vanuatu (RBV) under its [prudential framework](https://www.rbv.gov.vu/index.php/en/financial-stability/prudential-framework).

## Data

- **Types**: doctrine (prudential guidelines/policies), legislation (Acts & Regulations)
- **Documents**: ~37 across 5 categories
  - Prudential guidelines for domestic banks (12)
  - Prudential guidelines for international banks (12)
  - Prudential guidelines for insurance (5)
  - Legislation for the banking industry (5) — Financial Institutions Act CAP 254,
    International Banking Act CAP 280, AML/CFT Act, amendment acts
  - Legislation for insurance (5) — Insurance Act, regulations, amendments
- **Format**: PDF → extracted text (pdfminer)
- **Language**: English

A couple of insurance amendment PDFs are scanned images with no extractable text
layer; these return zero characters and are skipped automatically.

Generic anchor labels such as "Prudential Guideline 8" are enriched with the
document subject read from the PDF header (e.g. "Prudential Guideline 8 —
Restrictions on Exposures Greater Than 25 per Cent of Capital").
