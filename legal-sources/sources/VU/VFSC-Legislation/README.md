# VU/VFSC-Legislation

Financial services legislation administered by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), from [vfsc.vu/legislation](https://www.vfsc.vu/legislation/).

Covers ~154 PDF documents including consolidated acts, amendments, regulations, and guidelines in English and French. Key areas: companies law, international companies, financial dealers licensing, AML/CFT, e-business, partnerships, foundations, mutual funds, unit trusts, and virtual asset service providers.
