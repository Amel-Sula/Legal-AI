# NA/NAMFISA-Instruments

Namibia Financial Institutions Supervisory Authority — Regulatory Instruments

## Overview

Regulatory instruments published by NAMFISA across all regulated industries:
short-term and long-term insurance, pension funds, medical aid funds, capital
markets, microlending, and friendly societies. Includes Acts, regulations,
standards, directives, circulars, and Government Gazette notices.

452 unique documents discovered across 8 industry pages. Documents are PDFs
served via the WordPress Download Manager plugin. Full text extracted with
pdfplumber.

## Data Access

HTML scraping of industry pages to collect `data-downloadurl` attributes from
wpDataTables/WPDM tables. Each download URL returns a PDF directly.
