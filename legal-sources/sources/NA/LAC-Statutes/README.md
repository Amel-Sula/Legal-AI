# NA/LAC-Statutes — Namibia Legal Assistance Centre Annotated Statutes

Annotated statutes of Namibia from the [Legal Assistance Centre](https://www.lac.org.na/) (LAC), a public interest law centre providing free access to Namibia's laws.

- **Coverage:** ~500 annotated statutes (all laws in force as of January 2026)
- **Format:** Word (.docx) and PDF; text extracted from Word documents
- **Update frequency:** Statutes are annotated and updated periodically by the LAC
- **Language:** English
