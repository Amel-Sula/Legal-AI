# TJ/NBT-Regulations

**National Bank of Tajikistan — Banking Laws & Regulations**

Banking laws, regulations, instructions, and normative acts published by
the National Bank of Tajikistan at nbt.tj.

## Data

- **Banking Laws**: Laws on the National Bank, credit institutions, LLCs
- **Instructions & Regulations**: ~90 normative instructions for financial institutions
- **Insurance Laws**: Insurance sector legal acts
- **Islamic Banking**: Regulatory acts for Islamic banking
- **Payment Services**: Non-credit payment organization regulations
- **Draft Legal Acts**: Proposed legislation
- **Chart of Accounts**: Banking chart of accounts

Documents available in Tajik, Russian, and English. ~200 PDFs total.
