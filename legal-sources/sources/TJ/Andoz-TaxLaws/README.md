# TJ/Andoz-TaxLaws

**Tax Committee of Tajikistan — Tax Legislation**

Tax legislation, codes, laws, decrees, resolutions, and committee orders
published by the Tax Committee of Tajikistan at andoz.tj.

## Data

- **Tax Code**: Multiple editions (2010–2025) in Tajik + English translation
- **Laws**: ~30+ laws on banking, securities, accounting, investment, budget, etc.
- **Other Codes**: Civil, Criminal, Labor, Customs, Land, Water, Housing codes
- **Decrees**: Presidential decrees related to taxation
- **Resolutions**: Government resolutions (~53 documents)
- **Committee Orders**: Tax Committee orders (~59 documents)
- **Constitution**: Constitution of the Republic of Tajikistan
- **International Agreements**: Double taxation treaties

PDFs are hosted on andoz.tj. Most documents are in Tajik; one English
translation of the Tax Code is available. ~190 total documents.
