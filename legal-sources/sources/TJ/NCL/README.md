# TJ/NCL — National Center of Legislation (Tajikistan)

Official legislation database of the Republic of Tajikistan, maintained by the
National Center of Legislation (Маркази миллии қонунгузорӣ) under the President.

- **URL**: https://mmk.tj (Tajik) / https://ncl.tj (English) / https://ncz.tj (Russian)
- **Data**: Laws, codes, presidential decrees, government resolutions
- **Language**: Tajik (primary), some Russian
- **Format**: HTML scraping of Drupal 7 CMS
