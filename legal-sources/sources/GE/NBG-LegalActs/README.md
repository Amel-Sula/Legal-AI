# GE/NBG-LegalActs — Georgia National Bank Legal Acts & Regulations

Legal acts, orders, decrees and regulations issued by the National Bank of Georgia (NBG). Covers banking supervision, payment systems, securities market, monetary policy, consumer protection, sustainable finance, and other regulatory domains.

## Data

- ~84 English + ~319 Georgian regulatory documents
- Document types: Decrees, Orders, Regulations, Laws
- Full text extracted from PDF attachments via pdfplumber
- API endpoint: `GET /gw/api/pg/pages/static/legalacts` (paginated JSON)
