# ZM/SEC-Directives — Zambia Securities and Exchange Commission

Capital markets directives, rules, guidelines, circulars, guidance notes,
and tribunal decisions from the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) of
Zambia. Also includes the Securities Act No. 41 of 2016 (as amended).

## Data access

Uses the WordPress REST API (`/wp-json/wp/v2/posts`) to discover posts by
category, then extracts PDF links from post content. PDFs are downloaded
and full text is extracted via pdfplumber.

## Categories

- Directives (5) — binding instructions for regulated entities
- Rules (14) — statutory instruments on licensing, fees, conduct
- Guidelines (11) — ETFs, private funds, regulatory sandbox, internal controls
- Circulars (19) — reporting calendars, compliance notices
- Guidance Notes (6) — market guidance on fees, complaints, fines
- Capital Markets Tribunal (9) — tribunal decisions and orders
