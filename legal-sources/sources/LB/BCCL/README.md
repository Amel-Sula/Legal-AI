# LB/BCCL — Banking Control Commission of Lebanon

Banking regulatory circulars issued by the BCCL, covering capital adequacy,
AML/CFT, reporting, governance, and financial supervision.

- **Documents**: ~90+ circulars (1967-2025)
- **Language**: Arabic (PDF full text), English (metadata/descriptions)
- **Format**: PDF with pdfplumber extraction
- **Addressees**: Banks, Financial Institutions, Exchange Institutions, External Auditors
