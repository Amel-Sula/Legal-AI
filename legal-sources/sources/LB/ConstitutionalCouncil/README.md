# LB/ConstitutionalCouncil — Lebanon Constitutional Council Decisions

Decisions of the Lebanese Constitutional Council (المجلس الدستوري اللبناني), including constitutionality review rulings and parliamentary election dispute decisions.

## Source

- **URL:** https://cc.gov.lb
- **Type:** Case law (constitutional court decisions)
- **Language:** Arabic (some English translations)
- **Coverage:** 1994–present (~170 decisions)
- **Format:** PDF documents accessed via AJAX paginated listing

## Categories

1. **Constitutionality Review** — Challenges to the constitutionality of laws
2. **Parliamentary Election Disputes** — Challenges to election results
