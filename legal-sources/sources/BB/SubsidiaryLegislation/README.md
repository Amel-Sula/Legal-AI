# BB/SubsidiaryLegislation — Barbados Subsidiary Legislation

Subsidiary legislation (regulations, orders, rules, and statutory instruments) of
Barbados from the Office of the Attorney General & Legal Affairs.

- **URL:** https://oag.gov.bb/Laws/Consolidated-Laws/Subsidiary-Legislation-of-Barbados/
- **Coverage:** 400+ instruments, organized by chapter (CAP) number
- **Language:** English
- **Format:** PDF downloads, full text extracted via pdfplumber

Complements `BB/Statutes` which covers primary legislation (consolidated statutes).
