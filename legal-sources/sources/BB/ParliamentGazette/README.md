# BB/ParliamentGazette — Barbados Official Gazette (Parliament)

Official Gazette of Barbados, published by the Barbados Parliament. Contains Acts,
Statutory Instruments, Bills, government notices, trademarks, and other official
publications. Available from 2013-2026 with ~2,550 gazette issues.

- **Part A**: Acts, Statutory Instruments, Bills
- **Part B**: Notices, Trademarks
- **Part C**: Miscellaneous

Source: https://www.barbadosparliament.com/gazette
