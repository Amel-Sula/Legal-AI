# KG/NationalBank-Regulations

National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic (NBKR) — banking supervision regulations, normative acts, and banking laws.

## Coverage

- **Normative acts**: ~30 regulations covering economic ratios, capital requirements, credit risk, internal controls, AML/CFT, enforcement actions, Islamic banking principles
- **Banking laws**: ~14 laws (constitutional law on NBKR, banking law, licensing, credit unions, etc.)
- **Language**: English translations from the NBKR website

## Data Access

HTML scraping of JSP pages at nbkr.kg. Full text is embedded directly in HTML — no PDF extraction needed.

- Index pages: `index1.jsp?item=101&lang=ENG` (normative acts), `index1.jsp?item=40&lang=ENG` (laws)
- Detail pages: `contout.jsp?item=103&lang=ENG&material={ID}`
