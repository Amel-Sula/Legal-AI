# CD/DroitCongolais

**Droit Congolais — DRC Legislation and Case Law Portal**

Systematic collection (Recueil systématique du droit congolais) of Democratic Republic of Congo legal texts, maintained by an NGO. Covers legislation, international treaties, administrative decisions, and case law across 9 legal domains and 26 provinces.

- **URL**: https://www.droitcongolais.info/
- **Language**: French
- **Coverage**: 1886–present
- **Data types**: legislation, case_law
- **Format**: PDF documents, text extracted via pdfplumber
