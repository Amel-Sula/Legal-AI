# JM/FTC-Decisions — Jamaica Fair Trading Commission

Competition law enforcement documents from the Jamaica Fair Trading Commission (JFTC).

## Coverage

- **Case Reports** — staff investigation reports on mergers, acquisitions, and anti-competitive conduct
- **Judgments** — court rulings in FTC enforcement cases (Supreme Court, Court of Appeal, Privy Council)
- **Consent Agreements** — negotiated settlements with companies
- **Market Studies** — sector analyses (telecommunications, fuel, cement, etc.)
- **Advisories** — consumer and business guidance
- **Statements of Non-Objection** — merger clearance letters
- **Legislation** — the Fair Competition Act and related statutes

## Data Access

HTML scraping of WordPress site + PDF text extraction via pdfminer.
No API available. Public access, no authentication required.
