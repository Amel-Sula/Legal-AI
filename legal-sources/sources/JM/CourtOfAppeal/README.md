# JM/CourtOfAppeal — Jamaica Court of Appeal Judgments

Fetches civil and criminal judgments from the Jamaica Court of Appeal.

- **Source**: https://www.courtofappeal.gov.jm
- **Coverage**: 1990–2026
- **Data type**: case_law
- **Language**: English
- **Format**: PDF (full text extracted via pdfplumber)

## Categories

- Civil Judgments
- Criminal Judgments
- Civil Endorsements & Memoranda
- Criminal Endorsements & Memoranda
