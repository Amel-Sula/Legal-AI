# JM/BOJ-Regulations — Bank of Jamaica

Legislation (acts, regulations, codes of conduct) and supervisory guidance
(standards of sound practice, consultation papers, AML guidance notes) from
the Bank of Jamaica.

## Coverage

- **Legislation**: Bank of Jamaica Act, Banking Services Act, Building Societies Act,
  Payment Clearing and Settlement Act, Microcredit Act, Credit Reporting Act,
  Deposit Insurance Act, Proceeds of Crime Act, Terrorism Prevention Act, etc.
- **Supervisory Guidance**: Standards of sound practice (corporate governance,
  cyber risk, capital adequacy, fitness & propriety, agent banking, problem assets),
  Basel III framework, AML/CFT guidance notes, consultation papers, circulars.
- ~70 PDF documents total
- English language
- Full text extracted from PDFs

## Data Source

- Legislation: https://boj.org.jm/about-boj/legislation/
- Guidance: https://boj.org.jm/core-functions/financial-system/supervisory-guidance-notes-reporting-requirements/
