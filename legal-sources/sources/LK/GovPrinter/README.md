# LK/GovPrinter — Sri Lanka Department of Government Printing

Acts of Parliament from Sri Lanka's Department of Government Printing (1981–2026).

Each act is available as PDF in three languages (English, Sinhala, Tamil).
This scraper fetches English versions and extracts full text via pdfplumber.

## Data

- **Type:** legislation (Acts of Parliament)
- **Coverage:** 1981–2026 (~35 acts/year, ~1,500+ total)
- **Language:** English (Sinhala and Tamil also available on source)
- **Format:** PDF → extracted text
