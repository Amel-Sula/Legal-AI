# HT/BRH-Regulations — Banque de la République d'Haïti

Circulars, banking laws, prudential standards, guidelines, and lettre-circulaires
from the Central Bank of Haiti.

## Coverage

- **Circulaires**: ~50 circulars on banking supervision, AML/CFT, credit, payments, microfinance
- **Lettre-circulaires**: ~11 supplementary directives
- **Lois Bancaires**: Banking laws (commercial banks, savings banks, 2012 banking law, cooperatives)
- **Guidelines**: AML/CFT guidelines, financial institution directives
- **Notes additionnelles**: Supplementary notes to circulars

Some newer PDFs are scanned images without text layers and are skipped. French language.
