# LA/LaoTradePortal — Lao Trade Information Portal (Legal Documents)

Trade-related legal documents from the Lao PDR Trade Information Portal,
maintained by the Ministry of Industry and Commerce.

**Coverage:** ~1000+ documents including laws, decrees, decisions, notifications,
orders, regulations, instructions, provisions, and edicts — in English translation.

**URL:** https://www.laotradeportal.gov.la/en-gb/legal-document/index
