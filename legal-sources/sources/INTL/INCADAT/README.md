# INTL/INCADAT — International Child Abduction Database

INCADAT is maintained by the Permanent Bureau of the Hague Conference on
Private International Law (HCCH). It contains leading judicial decisions from
courts worldwide on the 1980 Hague Convention on the Civil Aspects of
International Child Abduction.

- **Type:** case_law
- **Languages:** Summaries in EN/FR/ES; full text in original language (PDF)
- **Volume:** ~1,700 cases (IDs 2-1711)
- **Coverage:** Courts from 60+ countries
