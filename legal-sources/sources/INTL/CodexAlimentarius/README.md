# INTL/CodexAlimentarius

**Codex Alimentarius — FAO/WHO International Food Standards**

International food safety and quality standards, guidelines, and codes of practice
adopted by the Codex Alimentarius Commission (joint FAO/WHO body).

- **Standards (CXS):** ~213 commodity and general standards
- **Guidelines (CXG):** ~86 guidelines on various food safety topics
- **Codes of Practice (CXC):** ~55 codes of hygienic and technological practice

Full text extracted from official PDFs hosted on the FAO website.
