# INTL/INTERPOL-CCF

INTERPOL Commission for the Control of INTERPOL's Files (CCF) — anonymized decision excerpts.

The CCF is the independent oversight body that reviews complaints about data processed through INTERPOL's information systems (Red Notices, diffusions, databases). It publishes anonymized decision excerpts covering topics such as Article 2 (human rights), Article 3 (political offences), due process, ne bis in idem, extradition, and data quality.

- **Coverage:** 2017–2025
- **Records:** ~65 anonymized decision excerpts (PDFs)
- **Type:** case_law
- **Language:** English
