# INTL/EACCA — East African Community Competition Authority

Legal framework documents from the East African Community Competition Authority (EACCA/EACA),
the regional competition enforcement body for EAC partner states (Burundi, DRC, Kenya,
Rwanda, South Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda).

## Data types

- **legislation**: Competition Act 2006, Amendment Act 2023, regulations, rules
- **doctrine**: Guidelines, notices, memoranda of understanding

## Method

Scrapes the EACCA legal framework pages for PDF links across categories
(competition-act, regulations-and-rules, gazettes, guidelines, mous).
Downloads PDFs and extracts full text via pdfplumber.
