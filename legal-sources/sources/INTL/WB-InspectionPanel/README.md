# INTL/WB-InspectionPanel

World Bank Inspection Panel — Cases & Reports

The Inspection Panel is the independent complaints mechanism for people and
communities who believe they have been harmed by World Bank-funded projects.
Established in 1993, it reviews complaints, investigates compliance with Bank
policies, and publishes investigation reports and recommendations.

## Data

- ~420 documents (1994–present)
- Document types: Investigation Reports, Reports & Recommendations, Notices of Registration
- Full text retrieved via the World Bank Documents & Reports API (`search.worldbank.org`)
