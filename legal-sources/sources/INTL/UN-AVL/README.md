# INTL/UN-AVL

UN Audiovisual Library of International Law — scholarly introductory notes on major
international legal instruments.

## Coverage

- 100+ instruments across 19 subject areas (criminal law, human rights, environmental
  law, law of the sea, disarmament, etc.)
- Each entry includes an expert introductory note by a recognized international law scholar
- Covers treaties, conventions, GA resolutions, declarations, and ILC draft articles

## Data Source

HTML pages at legal.un.org/avl/ with supplementary PDF introductory notes.
Text extracted from HTML `TabbedPanelsContent` divs; falls back to PDF extraction
via pdfplumber when HTML text is insufficient.
