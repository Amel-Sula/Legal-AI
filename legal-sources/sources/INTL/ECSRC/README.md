# INTL/ECSRC — Eastern Caribbean Securities Regulatory Commission

Securities legislation, regulations, rules, policies, and guidance notes from the
ECSRC. Covers the Securities Act 2001, 11 securities regulations, territory-specific
rules for all 8 ECCU member territories, and proposed legislation.

## Coverage

- **Acts**: Securities Act 2001, Amendment Acts
- **Regulations**: Conduct of Business, Prospectus, Collective Investment Schemes,
  Advertisements, Discipline, Licences & Fees, etc.
- **Rules**: Accounting & Financial Reports, Registration Statement (per territory),
  Corporate Governance, Repurchase Agreements
- **Guidelines**: Electronic Filing, Compliance & Internal Control
- **Proposed Legislation**: Securities Bill 2020, Investment Funds Bill 2020
