# INTL/WHO-LegalInstruments

WHO Governing Bodies legal instruments — World Health Assembly (WHA) and Executive Board (EB) resolutions, decisions, and annexes from 1948 to present.

## Data Source

Uses the **WHO IRIS DSpace 7 REST API** to search the Governing Bodies community for English-language resolutions and decisions. Full text is extracted from DSpace TEXT bundles (pre-extracted from source PDFs).

- **Scope**: Governing Bodies community (`347f287d-d8af-4e21-85cc-0fba3730380c`)
- **Filter**: `subject=Resolutions and decisions`, `publisher=World Health Organization` (English)
- **Volume**: ~6,800 documents
- **Coverage**: 1948–present (WHA1 through WHA78+, EB1 through EB158+)
