# INTL/UNILEX — CISG & UNIDROIT Principles Case Law

UNILEX is a database produced by UNIDROIT (International Institute for the
Unification of Private Law) containing decisions from courts and arbitral
tribunals worldwide relating to:

- **CISG** — UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods
- **UNIDROIT Principles** — Principles of International Commercial Contracts

Each case includes metadata, English-language keywords and abstract, and
(where available) the full text of the decision in its original language.

- **Type:** case_law
- **Languages:** Multi-language (abstracts in English; full text in original)
- **Volume:** ~2,500 CISG cases (1988-2026)
- **Coverage:** Courts and arbitral tribunals from 60+ countries
