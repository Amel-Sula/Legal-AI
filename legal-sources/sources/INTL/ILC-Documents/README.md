# INTL/ILC-Documents

UN International Law Commission (ILC) — Draft Articles, Commentaries, Conventions & Final Reports.

## Coverage

- **Topics**: ~50+ completed and ongoing topics covering all areas of public international law
- **Document types**: Draft articles, commentaries, conventions, guiding principles, model rules
- **Time span**: 1949–present (76+ sessions)
- **Key works**: State Responsibility, Law of Treaties, Diplomatic Immunity, Crimes Against Humanity, Law of the Sea, etc.

## Data Access

Full text PDFs hosted at `legal.un.org/ilc/texts/instruments/english/`. The scraper:
1. Discovers all topics from the master index at `texts/texts.shtml`
2. For each topic page, extracts PDF links for draft articles, commentaries, and conventions
3. Downloads PDFs and extracts text via pdfplumber
