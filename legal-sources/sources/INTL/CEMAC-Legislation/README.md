# INTL/CEMAC-Legislation

CEMAC (Communauté Économique et Monétaire de l'Afrique Centrale) legal instruments.

Covers regulations, directives, treaties and conventions from the Economic and Monetary Community of Central Africa — 6 member states: Cameroon (CM), Central African Republic (CF), Republic of Congo (CG), Gabon (GA), Equatorial Guinea (GQ), Chad (TD).

Documents are in French. Full text is extracted from PDF files published on cemac.int.

## Data

- **Regulations & Directives**: ~60 documents covering aviation, taxation, trade, telecommunications, etc.
- **Treaties & Conventions**: ~8 founding treaties and conventions
