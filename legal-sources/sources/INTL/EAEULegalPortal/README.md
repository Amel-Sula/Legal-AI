# INTL/EAEULegalPortal — EAEU Legal Portal

Official legal portal of the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU).

## Coverage

- **Supreme Council acts** — decisions and statements of the Supreme Eurasian Economic Council
- **Intergovernmental Council acts** — decisions of the Eurasian Intergovernmental Council
- **EEC Commission acts** — decisions, recommendations, and dispositions (~8000+ documents)
- **International treaties** — EAEU treaties and protocols
- **CU/CES documents** — Customs Union and Common Economic Space legacy documents
- **Memoranda and statements**
- **Official announcements**

Court acts (category 161) are excluded — covered by INTL/EAEUCourt.

## Data Access

HTML scraping of listing pages at `docs.eaeunion.org/documents/{category_id}/` with
pagination via `PAGEN_1=N`. Detail pages provide metadata and file download links.

Full text extracted from DOCX attachments (preferred) or PDF files.

## Language

Russian primary. Some documents also available in Armenian, Belarusian, Kazakh, Kyrgyz, and English.
