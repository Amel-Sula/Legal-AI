# INTL/COMESA-Competition

COMESA Competition and Consumer Commission — Decided Cases.

The COMESA Competition and Consumer Commission (CCCC) enforces competition
and consumer protection regulations across 21 Eastern and Southern African
member states. This source collects decided merger, anti-competitive business
practice, and consumer protection cases from the Commission's public portal.

**Data**: ~327 decided cases (mergers, ACBP, consumer) with PDF decisions.

**Method**: Scrape the Tablesome table on the decided-cases page, follow
links to individual case pages, download and extract decision PDFs via pdfplumber.
