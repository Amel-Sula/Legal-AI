# INTL/COMESA-Court — COMESA Court of Justice

Judgments and rulings from the COMESA Court of Justice, the judicial organ of the Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (COMESA). Covers trade, investment, and employment disputes from 21 member states.

- **Coverage:** ~38 decisions (2000–present)
- **Jurisdiction types:** First Instance Division, Appellate Division
- **Decision types:** Judgments, rulings, orders
- **Full text:** Extracted from official PDF documents
