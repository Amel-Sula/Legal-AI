# INTL/ERCA-Competition

ECOWAS Regional Competition Authority (ERCA/ARCC) — decisions, legal instruments, official journals, and guidelines.

ERCA enforces competition law across 15 West African ECOWAS member states. It became fully operational in October 2024 and handles merger control notifications.

## Data Types

- **case_law**: Merger control decisions (e.g., acquisition approvals with market analysis)
- **legislation**: Supplementary Acts, Regulations, Enabling Rules, Directives
- **doctrine**: Official Journals, merger guidelines, market study reports, strategic plans

## Method

- HTML post scraping for decisions and press releases (sitemap-based discovery)
- PDF download and text extraction for legal instruments (WordPress File Download plugin)
