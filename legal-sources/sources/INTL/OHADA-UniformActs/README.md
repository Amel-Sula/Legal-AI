# INTL/OHADA-UniformActs

OHADA Uniform Acts — harmonized business law across 17 African member states.

## Coverage

- 10 Uniform Acts (commercial law, companies, securities, insolvency, arbitration,
  mediation, cooperatives, accounting/AUDCIF, carriage of goods, debt recovery)
- Revised OHADA Treaty (2008)
- CCJA Arbitration Rules (2017)
- 12 documents total

## Member States

Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Comoros, Congo,
DR Congo, Côte d'Ivoire, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Mali,
Niger, Senegal, Togo.

## Data Source

English PDF translations from ohadalegis.com. Text extracted via pdfplumber.
The AUDCIF (accounting act) is available in French only.
