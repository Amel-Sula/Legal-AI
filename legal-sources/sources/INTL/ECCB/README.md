# INTL/ECCB — Eastern Caribbean Central Bank Regulations & Standards

**Source:** [https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/regulations-and-standards](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/regulations-and-standards)
**Data types:** legislation

Prudential standards, banking regulations, and guidance notes from the
Eastern Caribbean Central Bank (ECCB), applicable to all ECCU member states
(Antigua & Barbuda, Dominica, Grenada, St Kitts & Nevis, Saint Lucia,
St Vincent & the Grenadines, Montserrat, Anguilla).
