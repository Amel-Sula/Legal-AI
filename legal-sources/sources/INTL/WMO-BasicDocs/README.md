# INTL/WMO-BasicDocs

World Meteorological Organization — Convention and Technical Regulations.

Fetches the core WMO governance instruments with full text extracted from official PDFs:

- **WMO-No. 15** (Basic Documents No. 1, 2025 edition): Convention, General Regulations,
  Staff Regulations, Financial Regulations, UN Agreement, Privileges Convention,
  Swiss HQ Agreement — split into 7 individual records.
- **WMO-No. 49** (Technical Regulations): Volume I (General Meteorological Standards),
  Volume II (Aeronautical Meteorology), Volume III (Hydrology) — 3 records.

Total: ~10 documents, ~700K+ characters of full text.
