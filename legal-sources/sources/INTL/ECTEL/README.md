# INTL/ECTEL — Eastern Caribbean Telecommunications Authority

Telecommunications legislation and regulations from ECTEL member states:
Dominica, Grenada, St Kitts & Nevis, Saint Lucia, and St Vincent & the Grenadines.

Includes Telecommunications Acts, ~20 subsidiary regulations per country (spectrum,
licensing, interconnection, fees, etc.), the ECTEL Treaty, and the Electronic
Communications Bill 2020.

Full text extracted from official PDFs hosted on ectel.int via WordPress ACF API.
