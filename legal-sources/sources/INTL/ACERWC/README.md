# INTL/ACERWC — African Committee of Experts on the Rights and Welfare of the Child

Decisions on Communications and General Comments from the ACERWC, the African Union
treaty body established under the African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child.

- **Communications**: ~28 quasi-judicial decisions on individual complaints (case_law)
- **General Comments**: 10 interpretive guidance documents (doctrine)
- **Source**: https://www.acerwc.africa/
- **Full text**: Extracted from PDFs hosted on acerwc.africa
