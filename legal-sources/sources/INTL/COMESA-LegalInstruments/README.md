# INTL/COMESA-LegalInstruments

COMESA (Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa) legal instruments:
treaty, protocols, regulations, and official gazette volumes.

- **Treaty & Protocols**: from [comesacourt.org](https://comesacourt.org/comesa-treaty/)
- **Regulations**: from [comesacourt.org](https://comesacourt.org/comesa-regulations/)
- **Official Gazettes**: from [comesa.int](https://www.comesa.int/)

All documents are PDFs with extractable text (not scanned images).
