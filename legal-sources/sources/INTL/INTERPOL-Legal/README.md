# INTL/INTERPOL-Legal — INTERPOL Constitution and Legal Framework

Core governance instruments of the International Criminal Police Organization (INTERPOL),
including the Constitution, General Regulations, Rules of Procedure, and related documents.

## Data Coverage

- **Constitution** (2024 edition) — founding instrument
- **General Regulations** (2025) — operational framework
- **Rules of Procedure** — for the General Assembly and Executive Committee
- **Financial Regulations** (2024)
- **Rules on the Processing of Data** (2025) — data protection framework
- **Statute of the CCF** — Commission for the Control of INTERPOL's Files
- **Repository of Practice** on Articles 2 and 3
- **Terms of Reference** for Regional Conferences
- **Implementing Rules** on Settlement of Disputes

~11 official documents, all in English PDF format.

## Data Access

Direct PDF downloads from `interpol.int/content/download/`. Text extracted using pdfminer.
