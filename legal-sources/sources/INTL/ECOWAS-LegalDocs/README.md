# INTL/ECOWAS-LegalDocs

ECOWAS (Economic Community of West African States) legal instruments: treaties, protocols, supplementary protocols, conventions, directives, decisions, and supplementary acts.

- **Source**: https://www.ecowas.int/publication_category/legal-texts/
- **Coverage**: 15 West African member states
- **Documents**: ~94 legal instruments (PDFs)
- **Data type**: legislation
- **Format**: PDF → text via pdfplumber
