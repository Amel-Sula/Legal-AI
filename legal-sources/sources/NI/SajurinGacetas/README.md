# NI/SajurinGacetas — Nicaragua Official Gazette Archive

Digital archive of La Gaceta (Diario Oficial de Nicaragua) from the Enrique Bolaños
Foundation. ~20,211 gazette issues spanning 1940s through 2015+.

## Coverage

- La Gaceta — Official gazette of Nicaragua
- Laws, presidential decrees, official announcements
- Date range: 1940s - 2015+
- Language: Spanish
- PDF format with extractable text layers

## Access Method

HTML scraping of collection listing pages with paginated results (15/page).
PDF download and text extraction via pdfplumber.
