# UA/NAZK — National Agency on Corruption Prevention

Ukraine's National Agency on Corruption Prevention (НАЗК / NAZK) publishes
open data on [data.gov.ua](https://data.gov.ua/organization/natsionalne-ahentstvo-z-pytan-zapobihannia-koruptsiyi).

This source fetches two datasets:

1. **Unified State Register of Corruption Offenders** (~50,000 records)
   - Offense descriptions and punishment details from court decisions
   - Includes court name, case number, legal articles violated
   - JSON bulk download from data.gov.ua

2. **NAZK Monitoring Conclusions** (~170 records)
   - Conflict-of-interest violation findings by NAZK
   - Includes person/position, violation description, legal qualification
   - CSV from data.gov.ua
