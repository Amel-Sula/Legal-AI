# UA/NBU-Regulations — National Bank of Ukraine Regulatory Acts

Regulatory acts of the National Bank of Ukraine (NBU), including resolutions, decisions, regulations, instructions, and orders.

## Data Source

Documents are fetched via the NBU legislation search endpoint at `bank.gov.ua/ua/legislation`. Full text is extracted from PDF files hosted on the NBU website.

## Coverage

- **Temporal**: 2000s – present
- **Volume**: ~2,900 documents
- **Language**: Ukrainian
- **Types**: Resolutions (2,120), Decisions (251), Council Decisions (158), Notices (142), Orders (94), and others
