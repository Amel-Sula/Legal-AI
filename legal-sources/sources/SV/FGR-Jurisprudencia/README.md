# SV/FGR-Jurisprudencia

El Salvador Attorney General (Fiscalía General de la República) — Legislation & Jurisprudence Portal.

**URL:** https://jurisprudenciaylegislacion.fgr.gob.sv/

## Coverage

- **Legislation:** International instruments (118), national legislation (498), internal regulations (11)
- **Jurisprudence:** Constitutional, supranational, criminal, electoral, family, labor, administrative, and environmental case law (331)
- **Total:** ~785 unique documents with full text

## Method

Uses the WordPress AJAX API (`busqueda-archivos` plugin) with three actions:
- `materiasCategorias` — retrieve category hierarchy
- `docsPorCategoria` — list documents per category
- PDFs downloaded directly and text extracted via pdfminer
