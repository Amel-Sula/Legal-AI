# HU/MEKH — Hungarian Energy and Public Utility Regulatory Authority

Regulatory decisions (határozatok) published by the Magyar Energetikai és
Közmű-szabályozási Hivatal (MEKH).

Covers: energy licensing, pricing decisions, market integrity enforcement (REMIT),
utility regulation across electricity, natural gas, district heating, and water.

~96,800 documents available. PDFs downloaded and text extracted via pdfminer.
