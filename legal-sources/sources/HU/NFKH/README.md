# HU/NFKH — National Authority for Trade and Consumer Protection

Consumer protection and trade regulatory decisions (határozatok) published by
the Nemzeti Fogyasztóvédelmi és Kereskedelmi Hatóság (NFKH).

Covers: consumer protection enforcement, trade regulation, and related
administrative decisions issued by county government offices across Hungary.

~3,500 decisions available via Strapi CMS API. PDFs on Azure CDN, text
extracted via pdfminer.
