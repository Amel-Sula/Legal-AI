# VN/CongBao — Vietnam Official Gazette (Công Báo)

The Official Gazette of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, published
electronically at congbao.chinhphu.vn by the Office of the Government.

## Coverage

- **Documents**: ~25,000 legal acts
- **Types**: Laws (Luật), Decrees (Nghị định), Decisions (Quyết định),
  Circulars (Thông tư), Ordinances (Pháp lệnh), Resolutions (Nghị quyết),
  Consolidated Texts (Văn bản hợp nhất)
- **Language**: Vietnamese
- **Format**: Full text extracted from official DOCX files

## Data Access

Documents are listed via paginated HTML pages and downloaded as DOCX files
from the government CDN. No API key required.
