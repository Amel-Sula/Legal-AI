# IN/IBBI — Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India

Orders from courts at all levels (Supreme Court, High Courts, NCLAT, NCLT, DRAT,
DRTs, IBBI disciplinary) and regulatory documents (circulars, notifications,
guidelines) published by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India.

## Coverage

- **Court orders (case_law):** Supreme Court, High Courts, NCLAT, NCLT, DRAT,
  DRTs, IBBI disciplinary, IPA/RVO, other courts — ~40,000+ orders
- **Regulatory documents (doctrine):** Circulars (~97), Notifications (~150+),
  Guidelines (~50+)
- **Period:** 2016–present (since IBC enactment)
- **Format:** All documents are PDFs with text extraction

## Data Access

HTML tables at `ibbi.gov.in/orders/{court-type}` with paginated listings.
PDF URLs embedded in `onclick` handlers. No API required, no authentication.
