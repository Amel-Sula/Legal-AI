# IN/RajyaSabhaDebates — Rajya Sabha Verbatim Debates

Official verbatim debate transcripts from India's Rajya Sabha (Council of States),
the upper house of Parliament. Sourced from the Rajya Sabha Official Debates portal
at rsdebate.nic.in, maintained by the Rajya Sabha Secretariat and National Informatics
Centre.

## Coverage

- **Sessions:** 1–267 (May 1952 – present)
- **Records:** ~746,000 debate entries
- **Sub-collections:**
  - Part 1: Questions & Answers (~648K items)
  - Part 2: Other proceedings (~98K items)

## Data Access

The portal runs DSpace 5.6 without a REST API. Debates are accessed by:
1. Browsing by date with pagination (`/browse?type=debatedate`)
2. Fetching item pages for PDF URLs (`/handle/{handle_id}`)
3. Downloading text-layer PDFs (`PD_*.pdf`) and extracting text with pdfplumber
