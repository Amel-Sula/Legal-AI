# IN/NCLAT — National Company Law Appellate Tribunal

Judgments from NCLAT, India's appellate tribunal for company law and insolvency matters.

## Coverage

- **Benches**: New Delhi, Chennai
- **Case types**: Company Appeals, Competition Appeals, Insolvency Appeals, Contempt, Review, Restoration, Transfer
- **Period**: 2018–present
- **Format**: PDF orders (digitally generated, text-extractable)

## Data Access

Uses the display-board system at nclat.nic.in:
1. POST to `/display-board/judgement_details` with date range to get case listings
2. POST to `/display-board/view_order` with filing_no and order_date to download PDFs
