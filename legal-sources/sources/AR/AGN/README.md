# AR/AGN — Auditoría General de la Nación

Argentina's supreme audit institution (SAI), created by Law 24,156 (1992) and
enshrined in Article 85 of the Argentine Constitution. The AGN assists Congress
in external oversight of the national public sector.

## Data

- **Type:** Doctrine (audit reports, certifications, evaluations)
- **Records:** ~4,700+ resoluciones (1993–present)
- **API:** Drupal JSON:API at `webagnapi.agn.gob.ar`
- **Full text:** HTML body (`cuerpo` field) for recent reports; PDF extraction
  (ficha / informe) as fallback for older records with empty body text.
