# CA/AsyLex — Canadian Refugee Status Decisions

59,112 Canadian refugee status determination decisions (1996–2022) from the Immigration and Refugee Board of Canada (IRB/RPD), sourced via CanLII and published on HuggingFace by Claire Barale et al.

Decisions are anonymized (names replaced with XXXXXX). Full text of decisions preserved.

**Dataset:** [clairebarale/AsyLex](https://huggingface.co/datasets/clairebarale/AsyLex)
**Paper:** [NLP-informed Refugee Status Determination](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.469/) (ACL 2023 Findings)
