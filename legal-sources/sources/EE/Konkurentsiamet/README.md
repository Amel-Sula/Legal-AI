# EE/Konkurentsiamet — Estonian Competition Authority

Merger decisions and competition supervision case decisions from the
Estonian Competition Authority (Konkurentsiamet).

- **Merger decisions**: ~750 decisions since 2001
- **Competition supervision**: ~200 enforcement decisions since 2004

All documents are published as PDF transcripts with business secrets redacted.
