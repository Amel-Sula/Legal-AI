# MS/FSC — Montserrat Financial Services Commission Legislation

Fetches legislation from the Montserrat Financial Services Commission at
<https://www.fscmontserrat.org/legislations/>.

## Coverage

~54 legislative documents covering:
- FSC Act and amendments
- Banking, Trust, and Insurance Acts
- Anti-Money Laundering / Terrorist Financing regulations
- Companies Act and related regulations
- Securities and Investment Funds Acts
- Virtual Asset Business Act and regulations
- Intellectual property (Patents, Trade Marks)
- International cooperation legislation

## Strategy

1. Scrape the single legislation listing page for PDF links
2. Download each PDF from WordPress uploads directory
3. Extract full text via pdfplumber
