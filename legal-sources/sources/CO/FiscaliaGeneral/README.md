# CO/FiscaliaGeneral

**Fiscalía General de la Nación — Normatividad**

Normative documents from Colombia's Attorney General's Office: decrees, resolutions, directives, agreements, and laws. Approximately 186 PDF documents in Spanish covering salary regulations, institutional policies, criminal procedure guidelines, and more.

- **Source URL:** https://www.fiscalia.gov.co/colombia/la-entidad/normatividad/
- **Data type:** doctrine
- **Language:** Spanish
- **Format:** PDF (extracted via pdfplumber)
