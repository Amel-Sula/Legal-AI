# DO/SIPEN-Normativa

**Superintendencia de Pensiones (SIPEN)** — Dominican Republic pension regulator.

Fetches the full text of SIPEN's regulatory corpus:
- **Circulares** — technical specifications and operational instructions for AFPs
- **Resoluciones de la SIPEN** — binding decisions of the Superintendencia
- **Resoluciones de la CCRLI** — disability/occupational risk pension decisions

## Data

- ~970 documents (150 circulars + 520 SIPEN resolutions + 300 CCRLI resolutions)
- Full text extracted from PDFs via pdfplumber
- Published under Law 87-01 (Dominican Social Security System)
