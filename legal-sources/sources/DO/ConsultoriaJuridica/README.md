# DO/ConsultoriaJuridica

**Consultoría Jurídica del Poder Ejecutivo** — the Dominican Republic's most comprehensive legislation database.

Covers laws, decrees, regulations, resolutions, and Official Gazette entries from 1926 to present. Maintained by the Executive Branch's Legal Advisory Office.

- **URL:** https://www.consultoria.gov.do/consulta/
- **Documents:** ~90,000+ (estimated across all years and document types)
- **Full text:** Extracted from PDF documents via pdfminer
- **Types:** Leyes, Decretos, Reglamentos, Resoluciones, Varios, Gaceta Oficial
