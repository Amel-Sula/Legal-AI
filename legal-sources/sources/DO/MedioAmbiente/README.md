# DO/MedioAmbiente — Ministerio de Medio Ambiente y Recursos Naturales

Dominican Republic Ministry of Environment and Natural Resources — environmental
laws, decrees, norms, resolutions, and policies.

## Coverage

- **Laws (Leyes):** Ley General 64-00 (Environmental Law), forestry codes, water
  laws, mining laws, protected area declarations (~56 laws)
- **Decrees (Decretos):** Executive decrees on environmental protection, natural
  reserves, wildlife, waste management (~19 decrees)
- **Environmental Norms (Normas):** Technical standards for water quality, waste
  management, noise, radioactive waste, forestry, marinas (~12 norms)
- **Regulations & Policies:** Recent regulations and environmental policies (~16 docs)

## Data Source

Two pages on the ministry website list all documents:
- `/regulaciones/` — recent regulations and policies
- `/sobre-nosotros/marco-legal` — full legal framework

Documents are PDFs served via the WordPress WP File Download plugin.
