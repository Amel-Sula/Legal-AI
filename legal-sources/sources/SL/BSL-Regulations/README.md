# SL/BSL-Regulations

**Bank of Sierra Leone — Banking Regulations & Guidelines**

Banking acts, prudential guidelines, directives, and regulations published by the Bank of Sierra Leone (bsl.gov.sl).

## Coverage

- Banking Acts (BSL Act 2011/2019/2023/2024, Banking Act 2011/2019)
- Financial legislation (Companies Act, Bankruptcy Act, Payments System Act)
- AML/CFT regulations
- Prudential guidelines for commercial banks
- Guidelines for mobile money, leasing, micro-finance, foreign exchange
- Public notices and directives
