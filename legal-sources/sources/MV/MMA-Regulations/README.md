# MV/MMA-Regulations — Maldives Monetary Authority Regulations & Guidelines

Prudential regulations, banking and insurance guidelines, AML/CFT compliance
documents, and monetary policy rules issued by the Maldives Monetary Authority
(the central bank of the Republic of Maldives).

## Coverage

- **Acts**: MMA Act 1981, Banking Act, AML/CFT Act, National Payment System Act
- **Banking Regulations**: Capital adequacy, corporate governance, risk management, asset classification, Islamic banking, related-party transactions
- **Insurance Regulations**: Insurance industry regulations, broker/agent guidelines, prudential requirements
- **Monetary/FX**: Foreign currency regulations, cross-border declarations, exposure limits
- **Payment Systems**: Payment services regulation, mobile payments
- **AML/CFT**: Compliance guidelines for banks, securities institutions, money remittance

~34 English-language regulatory PDFs.
