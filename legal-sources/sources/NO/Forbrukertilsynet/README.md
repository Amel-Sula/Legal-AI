# NO/Forbrukertilsynet — Norwegian Consumer Authority

Enforcement decisions (vedtak) from the Norwegian Consumer Authority
(Forbrukertilsynet), covering prohibition orders, coercive fines, and
administrative penalties under the Marketing Control Act, Consumer
Contracts Act, and Transparency Act.

- **Coverage:** ~50 decisions from 2021 onwards
- **Language:** Norwegian (Bokmål/Nynorsk)
- **Update frequency:** Ongoing as new decisions are published
- **Data type:** doctrine
