# LS/LRA-TaxRulings — Revenue Services Lesotho

Public rulings, tax legislation, legal notices, customs & excise laws, VAT laws, and tax treaties published by Revenue Services Lesotho (formerly Lesotho Revenue Authority).

**Coverage:** ~100 documents across income tax, VAT, customs & excise, public rulings, tax treaties, and other revenue laws.

**Source:** [Revenue Services Lesotho](https://www.rsl.org.ls/) (formerly lra.org.ls)
