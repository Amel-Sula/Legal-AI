# LS/CBL-Regulations — Central Bank of Lesotho Financial Sector Regulations

Financial sector regulations, acts, guidelines, and notices from the Central Bank of Lesotho (CBL). Documents are published as PDFs on the CBL legislations page.

## Coverage

- **Acts**: CBL Act 2000, Financial Institutions Act 2012 (amended 2023), Insurance Act 2014, Payment Systems Act 2014, Pension Funds Act 2019, Data Protection Act 2011, Credit Reporting Act 2011
- **Regulations**: Banking (capital, liquidity, lending, licensing, AML/CFT), Insurance (solvency, licensing, reinsurance), Pensions, Capital Markets, Microfinance, Payment Systems, Financial Leasing, Consumer Protection
- **Guidelines**: KYC, AML, Cyber Security, Consumer Protection
- **Period**: 1987–2024
- **Language**: English
- **Format**: PDF (text-extractable via pdfplumber)
