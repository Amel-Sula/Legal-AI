# BW/CCA-Decisions

Botswana Competition and Consumer Authority (CCA) — Merger Decisions.

Competition merger review decisions from the CCA under the Competition Act 2018.

**Source:** https://www.cca.co.bw/index.php/merger-decisions

## Coverage

- 512+ merger decisions from 2015-2026
- Decisions on proposed mergers, acquisitions, and transfers
- Full-text PDF documents with legal analysis and determinations
- English language
