# BT/RMA-Regulations — Royal Monetary Authority of Bhutan

Prudential regulations, rules, guidelines and acts published by the Royal Monetary Authority (RMA) of Bhutan — the country's central bank.

**Coverage:** 70+ documents including prudential regulations, foreign exchange rules, payment system regulations, insurance rules, NPL management, corporate governance, and currency guidelines.

**Source:** [Legal Framework](https://www.rma.org.bt/legislation/) and sub-pages for FX, payments, and currency.
