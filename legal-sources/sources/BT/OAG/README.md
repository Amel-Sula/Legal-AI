# Bhutan Office of Attorney General - Acts

**Source:** [https://oag.gov.bt/language/en/resources/acts-2/](https://oag.gov.bt/language/en/resources/acts-2/)
**Country:** BT
**Data types:** legislation
**Status:** Complete

## Description

Acts of Parliament of the Kingdom of Bhutan, published by the Office of the Attorney General. Covers ~120 acts from 1953 to 2024, including foundational legislation such as the Constitution (2008), Penal Code (2004), Civil and Criminal Procedure Code (2001), and sector-specific statutes.

Available in English and Dzongkha. This fetcher prioritizes English versions.
