# BT/BICMA-Regulations

Bhutan InfoComm and Media Authority (BICMA) — ICT & Media Regulations.

Regulations, rules, guidelines, codes of practice, and standards governing
telecommunications, broadcasting, media, content, spectrum, and ICT in Bhutan.

**Source:** https://www.bicma.gov.bt/?page_id=242

## Coverage

- ICM Act of Bhutan 2018
- Rules on content, publication, printing press, cable TV, radio
- ICT facility and service regulations (ISP, MSO, infrastructure sharing)
- Spectrum management (frequency band plans, radio rules)
- Film regulations
- Cybersecurity code of practice
- Consumer protection, type approval, tariff orders
- Numbering plans, SIM registration, SMS codes of practice

~54 PDF documents, all in English.
