# CZ/UOHS — Czech Competition Authority Decisions

**Source:** [ÚOHS](https://uohs.gov.cz)
**Country:** Czech Republic (CZ)
**Type:** Case law
**Records:** ~10,000+ decisions

## Overview

The Office for the Protection of Competition (Úřad pro ochranu hospodářské
soutěže, ÚOHS) is the Czech competition authority. It publishes decisions on:

- **Public procurement** violations
- **Competition law** (cartels, abuse of dominance, mergers)
- **State aid** oversight
- **Significant market power** cases

## Data Access

JSON-LD open data index at `uohs.gov.cz/opendata/rozhodnuti.jsonld` with
structured metadata (case reference, participants, proceeding type, etc.).
Full text available on HTML detail pages and as PDF downloads.

Registered on [data.gov.cz](https://data.gov.cz).
