# CZ/ERU — Czech Energy Regulatory Office Decisions (ERÚ)

Binding decisions (pravomocná rozhodnutí) from the Czech Energy Regulatory
Office (Energetický regulační úřad). Covers energy sector supervision,
consumer protection in electricity/gas, license violations, and
administrative offenses.

- **Records:** ~5,700+ decisions
- **Language:** Czech
- **Period:** 2011–present
- **Full text:** Extracted from PDF attachments on each decision page

## Data Access

HTML scraping of `eru.gov.cz/pravomocna-rozhodnuti` paginated list.
Individual decision pages provide metadata and downloadable PDF files
containing the full decision text.
